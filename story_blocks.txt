# STORY BUILDING BLOCKS
# Ideas the program hands the narrator, a few at a time, chosen to fit the fight as it stands. They are
# suggestions to be reworded and woven in, never text to copy and never new events.
#
# HOW IT IS CHOSEN. Before each part of a beat the program looks at what is really happening (which parts were
# hit and how hard, how much each part hurts now, who is fresh and who is spent, who is pinned, what kind of move
# it was) and offers only blocks that fit. Among those it prefers blocks that have been offered least, and it
# will not offer the same block again for several beats. What has been offered is remembered between fights in
# blocks_memory.json (delete that file to start the count again).
#
# FORMAT. A line "== category | condition: values | condition: values" starts a group; every line under it is one
# block. A block can add conditions of its own after a "|" at its end. Lines starting with # are notes.
# Conditions (a block is offered only when every one of its conditions fits):
#   size:  glancing, solid, heavy, tremendous          how hard the blow landed
#   level: sore, hurting, very painful, excruciating, devastated     how much that part hurts afterwards
#   zone:  head, neck, chest, belly, back, arm, leg, tail            where on the body
#   has:   tails, tail, fins, horn, ruff, coils, arms, forelegs, legs, ears, antennae, wings, beak, talons, hood
#   state: fresh, winded, tired, spent                 her overall condition
#   kind:  claw, bite, charge, tail, beam, punch, horn, throw, grab, wing, beak, talon      what kind of move it is
#   role:  attacker, receiver, pinner, pinned, winning, losing
#   fade:  fighting, weakening, fading, nearly out      how far gone a pinned fighter is
#   posture: standing, down                          whether she is on her feet
#   held:  early, middle, long                        how long a pin has been held
#   part:  jaw, nose, eye...                          a word of the body part's own name
# In the text of a block, {part} is the body part in question ("left thigh") and {next} a part beside it.
# Add as many lines as you like: more blocks means more variety. /blocks shows the counts.

# kind: fire, ice, wind, rock (the element of a move, alongside its shape: a Fire Fang is bite and fire)

== aerial | moment: takeoff
she runs three steps, opens her wings and the ground falls away beneath the fourth | has: legs
a downdraft flattens the grass in a ring around the place she was standing
the first wingbeat is all effort; by the third she is riding the air instead of fighting it
she goes up steep and fast, out of reach before the other has finished turning
she climbs with her head turned down, never once taking her eyes off her opponent
dust and loose feathers swirl under her as she lifts, and then there is only the shadow | has: feathers
her wings open to their full span and the whole arena seems to shrink beneath them
she hangs at the top of the climb, wings spread wide, and lets the other one look up at her

== aerial | moment: dive
she tips over at the top of her climb and comes down like a dropped stone
the wings close tight against her body and she becomes a single falling line
the air screams past her and she aims the whole fall at one place
a flare of the wings at the last moment, and all the speed goes into the blow
she drops out of the sun and the other has to throw up a guard half blind
the dive comes in at a slant, low over the ground at the end, too fast to sidestep
she corrects once in the fall, a twitch of one wingtip, and the line is true again
the sound reaches the other a heartbeat before she does: a hiss of air over feathers | has: feathers

== aerial | moment: struck_from_above
the hit comes from above, where there is no way to brace for it
the weight of the whole fall lands on her at once and drives her down against the ground
she looks up too late: there is only a shape, and then the blow
it is like being hit by something thrown from a cliff
the air goes out of her in one push, as if the sky itself had stepped on her
she staggers under it and has to put a hand to the ground to stay up | has: arms
she flattens under the impact, legs splaying for a moment before they find the ground again | has: forelegs
she has no idea where it came from until the shadow slides off her

== aerial | moment: carried
the talons sink in and the ground drops away, and every wingbeat takes it further | has: legs
she kicks at nothing; her feet find only air | has: legs
the grip around her is the only solid thing in the world, and it is going to let go
she can feel each wingbeat above her as a lurch upward
the arena below wheels slowly, the trees small, the water a bright coin
for one long moment at the top of the climb neither of them moves at all

== aerial | moment: grounded
the hurt wing folds under her in mid-beat and the sky lets go of her
she tries to beat the bad wing and it only flutters, and she slides sideways out of the air | has: wings
the wing that has been carrying her on pain alone finally will not answer
she comes down in a long tilting spiral, the good wing hammering, the bad one trailing
the ground rises at her faster than she can sort out which way is up

== aerial | moment: knocked
the blow knocks her clean out of her wingbeat and she tumbles
she is spun round in the air, wings flailing for purchase that is not there
the hit stops her dead in the air, and for a moment she simply falls
she loses the air completely; the ground comes up in a rush of green and brown
feathers burst loose from where it struck her and drift down after her | has: feathers
she tries to catch herself with a wild beat of both wings and only half manages it | has: wings
the blow takes her out of the sky like a stone takes a bird off a branch
she is knocked sideways off her line, rolls in the air once, and comes down hard

== aerial | moment: strain
every downstroke drags at the hurt wing like a hook pulled through it
she flies lopsided, the bad wing beating short and late
she stays up on will alone, and every beat of it costs her
the wing trembles at the top of each stroke, as if it might simply stop
she holds the bad wing a little higher than the good one, favoring it even in the air
a feather tears loose from the hurt wing and spins away below her | has: feathers
her breath comes harder up here than it ever did on the ground
she knows she should come down; she does not

== movement | kind: fire
a breath drawn deep, and then the fire comes out of her like a door flung open
the flames roll across the gap and lick round her opponent from every side
the heat arrives before the fire does, a dry wall of it
a gout of flame, white at the heart and orange at the edges
the air shimmers and buckles between them as the fire crosses it
the fire spreads where it lands and clings, as if it were hungry
sparks scatter across the ground at the edges of the blast
the fire wraps round her opponent and for a moment there is only the glow inside it
her flame flares bright with the effort, sputtering high and white | has: flame
the heat on her own fur makes her eyes water, and she keeps pouring it out | has: forelegs

== movement | kind: ice
the air in front of her mouth turns white and the cold rolls out with it
frost creeps over the ground ahead of the beam, crackling as it spreads
the cold arrives with a sound like a pond freezing all at once
glittering crystals hang in the air behind the attack and drift slowly down
the beam is pale blue and so cold that it smokes
everything it touches turns white at the edges

== movement | kind: wind
her wings sweep forward together and the air comes with them like a wave | has: wings
the gust picks up leaves, grit and loose grass and throws all of it at once
the wind tears at her opponent's footing and pushes her back a step at a time
a spinning column of air whips up between them, full of debris
the blast of wind flattens the grass in a long fan in front of her
the air itself is the weapon: invisible until it lands, and then everywhere

== movement | kind: rock
stones lift from the ground around her and hang there for a heartbeat
the rocks come in a ragged volley, some high, some skipping off the ground
a jagged spike of stone erupts from the earth exactly where she means it to
she throws her head and the stones fly from her mane like shrapnel | has: mane
the rocks crack against each other in the air and come down in a hail of grit

== movement | kind: wing
she pivots on one foot and brings the wing across like a flail | has: wings
the wing comes down from above, the leading edge first, heavy as a branch | has: wings
both wings beat at once and she rises into the strike | has: wings
the wing is hard as steel along its edge, and she swings it like a blade | has: wings

== movement | kind: beak
she drives the beak in with the whole weight of her neck behind it | has: beak
the beak snaps shut an inch from where it was aimed, and the next one does not miss | has: beak
a quick double strike of the beak, faster than the eye can follow | has: beak

== movement | kind: talon
she comes in feet first, talons spread wide, wings braking hard above her | has: talons
the talons rake and catch and rake again | has: talons
she seizes with one foot and strikes with the other | has: talons
the grip of the talons closes like a trap and she leans back on it with her whole weight | has: talons

== movement | kind: punch
the fist comes round low and hooks up under her guard | has: arms
a short, chopping blow with the heel of the hand, the arm barely bending | has: arms
her clawed hand closes into a fist as it travels and lands like a hammer | has: arms

== reaction | size: solid, heavy
her feathers fluff out all over at once, then slowly settle | has: feathers
her crest snaps up rigid and stays there | has: crest
her wings jerk half open, as if to fly from it | has: wings
her beak gapes open on a sound that does not quite come out | has: beak
her tail flame flares high and flickers | has: flame
her tails lash out behind her, every one of them at once | has: tails
the rock spikes in her mane grate against each other as she flinches | has: mane

== reaction | size: tremendous
her wings fly open and she staggers, every feather on end | has: feathers
the flame on her tail gutters low and spits | has: flame
a scream tears out of her open beak, harsh and ragged | has: beak
her tails go rigid behind her, fanned out wide | has: tails

== pain_show | level: sore, hurting

== pain_show | level: devastated

== pain_show | level: hurting, very painful, excruciating, devastated | zone: arm

== tell | state: winded, tired

her flame burns lower than it did, steady but smaller | has: flame
her crest has stopped rising: it lies half flat now | has: crest

== tell | state: spent

== held_in | state: fresh, winded

# CRUSHED: a charge that drives her into something and keeps pushing (the receiver's side, sized to her condition)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
== crushed
her head snaps back and cracks against it a moment after her back does
the breath is squeezed out of her in one long push she has no say in, spit flying with it
there is no room to fall and no room to breathe: she is the thing caught between two hard things
her ribs bow inward and she can feel each one take the weight
the bark, the stone, whatever it is, prints itself into her back while the weight grinds into her front
her arms come up to push and find nothing to push against but the weight itself | has: arms
her hind feet scrabble at the ground for purchase and only slide | has: legs
the heat of the charge is still on her front, pressed into her with the weight | kind: fire
the water of the charge is still pouring over her face while she is pinned there, in her nose and mouth | kind: water

== crushed | state: fresh, winded
her mouth opens on a squeal that has no air behind it, only the shape of one
she clamps her jaw and takes it, eyes squeezed shut, every muscle locked against the pressure
a choked, voiceless gasp, and she will not give it more than that
she twists against it once, hard, testing, and it gives her nothing

== crushed | state: tired, spent
the sound that comes out of her is high and broken, all the air she had left
she sags into it, too spent to brace, and the weight does what it likes with her
her eyes roll and water; for a moment she can only hang there between the two
a thin whine leaks out of her with every bit of breath the weight lets her have
