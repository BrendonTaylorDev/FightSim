# STORY BUILDING BLOCKS
# Ideas the program hands the narrator, a few at a time, chosen to fit the fight as it stands. They are
# suggestions to be reworded and woven in, never text to copy and never new events.
#
# HOW IT IS CHOSEN. Before each part of a beat the program looks at what is really happening (which parts were
# hit and how hard, how much each part hurts now, who is fresh and who is spent, who is pinned, what kind of move
# it was) and offers only blocks that fit. Among those it prefers blocks that have been offered least, and it
# will not offer the same block again for several beats. What has been offered is remembered between fights in
# blocks_memory.json (delete that file to start the count again).
#
# FORMAT. A line "== category | condition: values | condition: values" starts a group; every line under it is one
# block. A block can add conditions of its own after a "|" at its end. Lines starting with # are notes.
# Conditions (a block is offered only when every one of its conditions fits):
#   size:  glancing, solid, heavy, tremendous          how hard the blow landed
#   level: sore, hurting, very painful, excruciating, devastated     how much that part hurts afterwards
#   zone:  head, neck, chest, belly, back, arm, leg, tail            where on the body
#   has:   tails, tail, fins, horn, ruff, coils, arms, forelegs, legs, ears, antennae, wings, beak, talons, hood
#   state: fresh, winded, tired, spent                 her overall condition
#   kind:  claw, bite, charge, tail, beam, punch, horn, throw, grab, wing, beak, talon      what kind of move it is
#   role:  attacker, receiver, pinner, pinned, winning, losing
#   fade:  fighting, weakening, fading, nearly out      how far gone a pinned fighter is
#   posture: standing, down                          whether she is on her feet
#   held:  early, middle, long                        how long a pin has been held
#   part:  jaw, nose, eye...                          a word of the body part's own name
# In the text of a block, {part} is the body part in question ("left thigh") and {next} a part beside it.
# Add as many lines as you like: more blocks means more variety. /blocks shows the counts.

#   guard moment: catch (a blow caught on a forearm, foreleg, wing, horn, coils), turn (turned aside)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
== guard | moment: catch
the guard comes up a heartbeat before the blow and takes it, and the shock runs all the way to her shoulder
she catches it on the hard outside of the limb, where there is more bone than anything else
it lands on her guard instead of where it was aimed, and still hurts, but nothing like it would have
she feels the force go into the guard and stop there, and lets out the breath she was holding
the block numbs the limb for a moment; she shakes it out and doesn't take her eyes off her opponent
her horn rings with the impact, and she sets her head against the force of it | has: horn
she takes it on a loop of her body, scales flexing, the force spread along the coil | has: coils
a wing comes round like a shield and the blow drives into the stiff feathers at its leading edge | has: wings

== guard | moment: turn
she meets it at an angle and it simply slides off her, wasted
she gives with it instead of against it, and it goes on past her into nothing
a touch at the right moment, no more, and the whole blow turns aside
she redirects it with almost no effort, and that is the insulting part
for an instant they are very close, and then the attack has gone wide and her opponent is stumbling past

== crash
there is nothing where she aimed, and all that speed has to go somewhere
she sees the obstacle coming and cannot stop, and turns her shoulder at the last moment to take it
the impact shocks back through her own body, teeth to tail
she hits it hard, harder than she meant anything to hit, and bounces off
for a moment after the crash she does not know which way she is facing
the rage of the miss is worse than the bruise, almost
dust and grit come down round her where she struck

# FEINTS, JUGGLES, LAST STANDS
#   feint moment: bit (the defender fell for it), read (she saw through it)
#   juggle moment: up (knocked into the air), caught (hit there and smashed down)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
== feint | moment: bit
the shoulder dips one way and every instinct she has goes with it, and the real blow is already coming from the other side
her eyes follow the fake, and by the time they come back it is far too late
she commits to blocking a blow that never comes, and is turned wide open for the one that does
a twitch of the head, a half-step, and she flinches from nothing, and pays for it
the feint drags her guard high, and the strike goes in underneath

== feint | moment: read
the fake is good, but she has seen it once already and her weight doesn't move
she watches the eyes, not the paw, and the eyes give it away
she doesn't bite; she simply waits for the real thing

== juggle | moment: up
the ground leaves her all at once and there is nothing under her paws at all
for a moment she hangs in the air, weightless, and has time to know she cannot do anything about what comes next
she is lifted clean off her feet, limbs flung out, the world tilting

== juggle | moment: caught
nothing to push against, nothing to brace with: the second blow takes her in the air and she simply goes where it sends her
she was still rising when it hit, and the force reverses her in mid-air and drives her down
the ground comes back up at her twice as fast as she left it

== last_stand
everything she has left goes into this, and she knows there will be nothing after it
a sound tears out of her as she throws it, half roar and half gasp of effort
she has been saving nothing, and now she spends it all at once
for one moment she is as strong as she was at the start, and then she is emptied out

== frenzy
she has stopped counting; her body just keeps doing it
the same place, again, again, until the spot is all there is
somewhere in it the blows stop being a choice and become a rhythm
she hears herself panting in time with it and doesn't stop
the first blows were aimed; the last ones are just hers, falling where the others fell
