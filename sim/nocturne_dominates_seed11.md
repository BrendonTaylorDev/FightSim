> **Superseded:** this run is from early in build 113, before the pin pressure cap, the health soft cap and the
> change that keeps pummels out of pins. See `seed10_transcript.txt` for the current build.

Build 113 (in progress). I played the director with the moves below. The engine rolled everything else: dodges,
how long each pummel ran, grip breaks, get-ups, the pin, and why she went out. I wrote the narration from the
narrator's real notes for each beat.

### Beat 1 — the chain

Nocturne did not circle. She walked straight across the dry ledge at the Buizel, head low, and at three strides
she checked, dipped her left shoulder as if to go right, and went left.

Ripples bought the feint with her whole body. By the time she corrected, a forepaw was already in her belly.

It was not a swipe. It was a short, flat punch with the full weight of the Absol's chest behind it, driven in
under the ribs where the cream fur was softest, and it folded her. The air went out of her in one ugly bark. Her
right hip took the edge of it and the thigh below jarred, and for a moment the whole front of her was one hot
knot she could not breathe around.

Nocturne was already past. Her horn came round on the way, low, and raked across the outside of the left thigh,
a hard, dragging line from hip to knee that Ripples felt open up a half-second after it happened. She hopped. She
could not help it. The leg wanted to fold and she would not let it, and her lower back caught the twist and
complained about it.

*She's fast. Fine. Fast I can do.*

She had just turned to face the white bulk again when the sickle tail came down across her back.

It hit between the shoulder blades with a flat, metallic weight, Iron Tail. It was not the worst of the three; the
blade of it rang more than it bit. But it pressed her chin to her chest and jarred up into the bare fur of her
neck, and she stood there for a heartbeat with her arms wide, fins flared, her tails thrashing for balance behind
her, while three different places told her about themselves at once.

Nocturne stopped a length away, turned, and looked at the belly.

*There.*

### Beat 2 — Aqua Jet

Ripples did not give her the second look.

Water came up out of nothing round her, off the pools and out of her own soaked fur, a skin of it closing over
her from nose to tails, and she went. Low, flat, the way she went through a river, and she hit the Absol square in
the chest.

The impact was only the start of it. Nocturne felt the small hard skull drive into her breastbone and her ribs on
the left, and felt the water go round her throat like a cold fist. Then she was going backward. Her paws were on
the stone and the stone was going past them. The Buizel never left her. There was no gap, no second strike; the
jet simply kept coming and kept her inside it, and the water did not stop working on her the whole way across the
ledge. It pressed. It stung. It scoured at her chest where the skull had struck and across her ribs and down both
flanks, a hard grinding sheet of it that she could not blink out of and could not step out of, the whole width of
the cave.

Her back found the stalagmite.

Stone took her across the spine and the right shoulder, and her left hip cracked against a knuckle of rock at its
base. It held. For a moment that was worse, because the Buizel and the water did not stop when the stone did. They
kept pushing, the same contact still driving in. Her chest was flattened between the jet and the rock, her ruff
was crushed into her own throat, and the water poured on over her face until there was nothing to breathe but it.

Then it was spent, and the small orange shape dropped back out of it, heaving.

Nocturne's legs decided for her. She went down on her side at the foot of the stalagmite, ribs working, a choked
cough dragging up out of a throat that felt as if it had been wrung.

*Hm,* she thought, from the floor. *Noted.*

Ripples stood over the pools with her chest going like a bellows and her arms hanging. The jet had cost her. She
could feel exactly how much.

### Beat 3 — breath

Nocturne put a forepaw on the stalagmite and came up in one ugly heave, the chest grinding, and stood.

Something cracked overhead. A short stalactite, loosened by the slam, let go of the ceiling and came down on
the Buizel's head on its way to the floor: across the right cheek and off the shoulder, enough to make her duck and
swear under her breath and shake her head like a wet dog.

They looked at each other across the ledge. Nocturne's breathing was shallow and careful, all of it high in her
chest, where it didn't have to go past the bruise. Ripples' was quick and loud, and her left leg had started to
shake when she stood still.

*The belly,* Nocturne thought. *And make her stand still for it.*

> A stalactite falls on Ripples: Right Cheek 38% 🟡 · Nocturne gets up first try · ❤️ Nocturne 1647 · Ripples 1632

### Beat 4 — the grab, and the pummel

She came in fast and low, and this time she did not go past. Her jaws closed on the Buizel's right upper arm, not
hard, just enough, and she set her paws and pulled.

Ripples came in against her chest whether she liked it or not. There was no room. There was a white shoulder in
her face and the jaw locked on her arm and nowhere to put her feet, and then the forepaw started.

Short blows. No wind-up, nothing to see coming, the claws raking into the belly that the first punch had already
made sore, and again, and again. The first one hurt far more than a blow that size should have. It found the old
bruise under the new one, and a cry got out of her before she could stop it; she bit it off short. She snatched
her middle away from it, as far as the jaws would let her, which was not far, and a hard shudder ran through her.
She set her jaw and held the next sound in. By the third she
could not tell where one ended. By the fourth the whole front of her was one bright, swollen ache with her
heartbeat in it.

The fourth came slower than the first. Nocturne had to breathe. She held on, heaving through her nose, the arm
still in her teeth.

### Beat 5 — point-blank

Nocturne did not let go of the arm. She turned her head a little against it, so that the Buizel's body swung
square in front of her, and opened her mouth around the grip enough to let the water out.

Hydro Pump at no distance. There was no gap between them for it to cross. It hit the ruined belly at the
thickness of a forearm and stayed there.

Ripples screamed. Not a yelp: a real one, short and torn. It was cut off when the water filled her mouth, then
started again when it eased. Water was her own element and it did not matter. The Absol held it on her: one pulse,
the belly; a second, the pressure grinding the hurt deeper instead of washing it out; a third, and it spread up
under the ribs into her chest and down across her left hip, the whole front of her pinned to the force of it
like a leaf to a grate; a fourth.

When it stopped she was still standing, because the jaws on her arm were holding her up.

She hung there with her head down and the water running off her, and every breath she took went in short and came
out shaking. Under the shaking, she was working her arm in the Absol's teeth, a quarter-inch at a time.

*You haven't got me. You've got an arm. I've got two.*

The grip loosened, a little. It did not open.

### Beat 6 — Ripples hits back

Her free arm came round.

It was a chop, edge of the paw, Brick Break, the one thing she owned that the Absol was soft to. She threw it
from inside the grip, at no distance, and there was nowhere for the dark face to go. It landed square across the
muzzle with a crack that rang off the cave wall, and the edge of it carried on into the left ear and down the side
of the neck.

Nocturne's head snapped sideways. Her eyes watered so fast that for a moment she could not see. Her nose filled
with the hot copper smell of her own muzzle and the ear rang, high and thin. She growled through the arm in her
teeth, a long, ugly sound from the bottom of her chest, and did not let go.

*Good,* she thought, very clearly, under the pain. *That's all you had.*

### Beat 7 — the knee

The sickle tail came round low and took the left knee from the side.

Iron Tail on the leg the horn had already opened. The knee went out sideways and the Buizel went down with it,
flat on her back on the ledge, the stone slapping her shoulders, her hip, the back of her head. On the way down
she did the one thing she could: she twisted, set her free paw against the white chest and shoved, and the jaws
came off her arm with a wet tearing of fur.

She lay on her back with the knee drawn up, breathing at the ceiling.

### Beat 8 — on the ground

Nocturne stood over her and bit down into the belly.

Not a pin. She simply put her head down and closed her jaws on the swollen cream fur, released, and closed them
again. She did it twice, then had to stop for air, her muzzle aching from the chop.

The first bite found everything the claws and the water had left behind. It went straight through Ripples
like a wire pulled tight from her navel to her throat, and a cry got out of her before she could stop it. She
still had most of her strength, so she clamped it off and caught her breath and held it. The second bite came
before the first had faded and added itself on top. The sound she was making caught and went higher.

She got up anyway. It took three tries. The first, her arms gave out under her; the second, the knee did. On the
third she got a paw onto a fallen boulder and hauled, and stood up crooked against it with one arm wrapped round
her middle and her tails hanging still.

*Up. Up is something.*

### Beat 9 — breath

Neither of them moved for a while. The cave dripped. Nocturne stood with her head low and her mouth open, getting
her wind back, the muzzle throbbing in time with her heart. Ripples leaned on the boulder and watched her, and
did not try anything, because she had nothing she wanted to try with.

> ❤️ Nocturne 1543 (86%) · Ripples 705 (41%)

### Beat 10 — the pin

When Nocturne came, she came round the boulder's side. She caught the Buizel's shoulder in passing with her own
and simply turned her, spun her half round by the weight of it, and when the bad knee would not take the turn,
down she went, onto her back on the stone.

Nocturne followed her down and placed herself. Her right hind paw on the left thigh, over the cut. Her chest and
the full wet weight of her ruff across the Buizel's chest. Last, her left forepaw, flat, pad down, on the belly.

She did not press yet. She let it rest there and watched the small black eyes understand where it was.

### Beat 11 — from on top

Then she raised the paw and brought it down.

Night Slash, short. From on top there was no room for anything else, and none was needed. Five times. The first
landed on a belly that was already ruined, and it tore a raw scream out of her. She was too worn now to keep any
of it in. Her whole body jerked up against the weight on her chest and was flattened again, and she shook, fine
and fast, from her ears down to her feet. The rest came before the scream had finished. Each one went in on top
of the last and pushed the sound she was making higher, until it cracked and was only breath.

Then the paw settled, and pressed.

She kicked. She got her good leg under her and shoved at the stone with everything left in it, and the Absol's
weight didn't even shift. The answer came down through the forepaw and into the belly, a slow deliberate push,
and the kick went out of her.

Something lit in her then, a last hot flare of it that came up from nowhere: her heart slammed, her vision
sharpened, her tails beat at the stone.

*Not like this. Not flat on my back like a fish.*

### Beat 12 — under

The surge gave her one good twist. She wrenched her shoulders to the left, hard, trying to roll the weight off her
chest, and the forepaw on her belly simply followed her and leaned.

The pain was worse than any blow had been. It did not come and go. It was there, under the pad, and it got
bigger when she breathed in, so she breathed less.

Above her Nocturne's face was very close. The muzzle she had split was swelling and there was dark crusting at
the nose. The red eyes were not angry. They were patient, as if they had all day.

> Struggle (5%): twists to one side, fails, punished · ❤️ Ripples 22 → −109 · going under 22 → 33%

### Beat 13 — bridge

She bridged. Heels, shoulders, and everything in between lifted off the stone. For one breath the Absol
actually rose with her, and then the chest on her chest came down again and drove her flat, and the weight that
had gone up came down on her ribs. The air went out of her and did not come back for a long time.

When it did, it came in small. Her hands had found the white fur of the forelegs and were holding on, the way you
hold the edge of something.

> Struggle (2%): bridges, fails, punished on the Chest (174→207% 🟣) · ❤️ Ripples −179 → −189 · going under 33 → 55%

### Beat 14

She didn't try this time. She lay and breathed in short pulls, as little as she could get away with, and the
cave's drip counted something she was not counting any more. Her tails had stopped moving. The pain under the
forepaw had stopped being a place and become the whole floor of her.

*Breathe. Little ones. Little ones are fine.*

Nocturne shifted her hind paw on the cut thigh, settling, and the Buizel's whole body jerked and went still again.

> No real attempt (66% to try, rolled 98) · ❤️ Ripples −268 → −331 · going under 55 → 68% (nearly gone)

### Beat 15

Nothing, for a while. Nocturne's own ribs were starting to ache in the long stillness, and she adjusted her
weight off the bruise on her chest and back onto the belly. Ripples made a thin sound at that and her eyes
half-closed. Each out-breath now closed them a little further, and each in-breath dragged them back open.

> No real attempt (55%, rolled 65) · ❤️ Ripples −340 → −403 · going under 68 → 93%

### Beat 16 — out

She tried once more. She got her right paw round the trapped left thigh and pulled at it, as if she could drag
her own leg out from under the Absol. The hind paw on it pressed back, and the thigh lit up all the way to the hip.

It was the belly that took her, in the end. The ache under the forepaw rose one last time and swallowed
everything else, the thigh, the stone, the cold water in her fur. Her eyes closed, and this time they did not come
back open. The hand on the Absol's foreleg loosened and slid off and lay on the stone with the fingers curled.

Nocturne stayed where she was and counted ten of her own breaths. She watched the cream belly under her paw go
up and down, slow and shallow and even. Nothing tensed, nothing tried.

Then she lifted the forepaw, and the hind paw, and took her weight off the narrow chest, and stood over the
Buizel in the grey light with her muzzle throbbing and her own breath loud in the cave.

*Good fight,* she did not say. She looked at the arm she had held, and the knee, and the belly, and then she
turned and limped to the dry end of the ledge and lay down where she could see her.

## Notes from this run

- **Aqua Jet** now stays on her the whole way. The narrator is told there's no gap, no second strike and no
  outpacing. The water's extra contacts on the chest, ribs and both flanks cost Nocturne about 44 of the
  charge's 143 health. At 40 energy (48 with the charge), it took Ripples from 100 to 52.
- **Body parts by vitality:** the Brick Break shows it best. The ear took 140% for −7 health, while the neck
  took 79% for −25.
- **Raw reactions follow her condition blow by blow.** At 96% strength the first pummel blow gets a cry she
  bites off and fights to keep in. At 64% the first ground bite gets the same, held in harder. At 41% the pin pummel gets a raw scream she
  can't hold in. Later blows in the same pummel build on the first reaction instead of each getting a new
  scream.
- **Balance to watch:** at `/scale 5` with numbness off, blows on a part that's already devastated (1.8× there)
  are huge. Two bites on her stomach cost 371 health, and the five-blow pin pummel cost 541. That fits "Nocturne
  devastates her", but in an even fight a pummel on a ruined part could end things fast. If that feels like too
  much, the fix is lowering `grapple.pummel_power` (0.6 now) or the 1.8× multiplier for parts at the bottom of
  their resistance.
