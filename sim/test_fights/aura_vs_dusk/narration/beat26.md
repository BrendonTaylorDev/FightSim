— Aura —

The wolf made a sound without meaning to: a small, wet click in her throat on the in-breath, like a latch that would not catch.

Aura heard it, and her feet found the mud again under her, one and then the other, square. Her guard came back up, as far as her stomach would let it.

The wolf held her head oddly still, as if balancing something on it. When the wind dropped a branch somewhere behind her, she flinched; anything at her back made her flinch now.

*She'll go left,* Aura wagered with herself. *Away from my good arm.*

A cold focus came down over her and narrowed everything to the stone mane. Not to hit it. To hold it. If she could get a paw into that mane, the wolf could not get away from anything.

She crowded in, taking away the room the wolf would need to turn, and reached. Her right knee gave a little as she landed on it, and she covered it with the reach itself, her whole weight going forward behind her paw.

— Dusk —

She did not go left.

She slipped right, one short step to the side, and let the paw go by. It passed a finger's width from the stone of her mane; she felt the air of it stir the wet fur on her neck.

For an instant they were side by side, close enough to touch, and the jackal's weight was still going forward after the paw that had caught nothing, and her face was turned the wrong way.

Dusk did not have the breath to do anything with it. She only stepped back out of reach. Mud sucked at a hind foot and let it go.

Her legs were shaking. Her throat was a ring of fire. Her back was one long bruise.

But some part of her was still ready, and she let that part stand in front of all the rest.
