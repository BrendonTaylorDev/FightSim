— Nocturne —

A breath of air came in through the gap between two leaning pillars and moved across her face, cool on the damp fur of her muzzle, carrying the green smell of the jungle outside. Nocturne lifted her nose into it for a moment and let it clear her head.

The otter had not moved. She stood with both arms wrapped across herself, the hurt right paw curled shut and tucked against her chest, the left arm laid over the top of it like a bar across a door. She could not stand straight. Something in that chest kept folding her a little forward at the middle, her shoulders hunched round it, her head low.

*She nearly had my shoulder. Nearly. I'll take it.*

A steadiness came over Nocturne, settling on her back like a hand. Her right ribs still burned with every breath and the left shoulder still ached where she had folded on it, but neither mattered much just now. The orange arm lay across the guard, right there, the upper part of it nearest to her, thick with wiry muscle under the short fur. It was holding everything else shut. Take it away and there was nothing behind it but what she wanted.

She came in low and quick, her weight going forward off her haunches, her ears flat to her skull and her lips peeling back off her fangs.

A snarl rolled out of her as she snapped forward. Bite.

— Ripples —

The teeth went into her left arm above the elbow with a hard wet snap, through fur and into muscle, and the force of the jaws drove the arm straight back into her own chest and slammed the fin along her forearm flat against her ribs, all of it at once.

The arm jerked out sideways as the white jaws let go, and a sound came out of her, *yipss*, a yip and a hiss tangled together.

Then her chest caught up.

It came a half-instant behind the bite and it was so much bigger. The old hurt there, where claws and the white one's charging head had already been, flared up white and filled her, and she screamed. It was a real scream, high and climbing, an *eeeeee* that went up off the pillars and into the open sky, and every bird outside the walls went quiet at once. Her whole body jerked in round it. She folded forward over her own arms, gasping, her ears dropped out to the sides, and her twin tails went stiff and straight out behind her and quivered there.

Somewhere along the forearm the fin stung where it had been knocked against her. She huffed at it through her nose, *fff*, and could spare it nothing more.

She tried to look up at Nocturne, and her head would only come halfway.

— Ripples —

For a while there was nothing in the world but her chest.

The hurt there seemed too big to be inside her. It stood round her instead, and she was inside it, and every breath had to go out through it and come back. Her breath came in ragged pulls, a rough sawing in and out, louder than anything else in the hall. She gulped at the air like water, and it was never enough.

Her fins had clamped flat to her forearms. Her tails stalled and hung. Down the backs of her legs the muscles jumped in little uneven twitches, and under the fur of her back the sweat had gone cold.

She looked down at herself. The cream fur of her chest was scored in tufts, puffed and dark beneath, and now a double row of tooth marks sat in the orange fur of her upper arm just above it, already swelling. The chest shook with every beat of her heart. It had been the very first thing the white one hit, right at the start, a shoulder driving into it before she had even set her feet. She could feel that first blow down there still, with all the others piled on top of it.

A squeak got out before she could clamp her jaw on it, and her cheeks burned.

She lifted the left arm an inch to test it. It came. Slowly, with a sharp complaint above the elbow and a dull one after, but it came, and she laid it back across her chest where it belonged.

Low behind it, her stomach ached, a sick deep ache under the sharper line of the old claw marks.

*Still here. Still standing. Look at her.*

She lifted her eyes to Nocturne's and held them there, and a lizard flicked across a sunlit flagstone between them and was gone into a crack.
