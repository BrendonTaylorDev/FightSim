— Ripples —

The air in the roofless hall had gone warm and close, heavy with wet moss, but her own skin felt hot and cold at once. Her chest burned under her crossed arms, and the fur along her back was clammy where the sweat had come up through it.

She looked at Nocturne for a long time.

The white one stood with her chin tucked down into her ruff, the thick white fur bunched up under her jaw, and between breaths her left shoulder rolled forward and back, a small careful testing movement. That was the shoulder Ripples had chopped at the very start of all this. Nocturne was checking it. She thought nobody saw.

*That one. Again.*

Ripples was afraid of the next exchange, and she hated that she was afraid. The fear sat in her stomach beside the ache there, and she was angry at it, and the anger was more use to her.

She let her left foot slide a little on a wet patch of flagstone, as if the moss had taken it, and her shoulders dipped with it. The red eyes followed the slip. In the same instant she came up out of it, drove from her hips, and the shoulder snapped through after, her right arm swinging high and the edge of her webbed paw coming down hard and flat at the white shoulder: Brick Break, everything in her small wiry body behind it.

— Nocturne —

She saw the slip and did not believe it.

The orange arm came up and over, and Nocturne dropped. Not back: down. Her forelegs folded and her chest went low to the stone, the horn ducked tight along her cheek, and the chop went clean over the top of her. She felt the wind of it part the tuft on her head. It passed so close that one hair of her ruff was dragged along with it.

The shoulder under her twinged as she folded on it, a dull complaint, and her right side gave a hot warning stab as the ribs bent. She ignored both.

The otter's arm had gone all the way through and kept going, and her paw came down past Nocturne's face, open, the webbing spread, nothing in front of it.

Nocturne came up out of the crouch and raked her claws across it as it went by.

Three claws, a short hard swipe from below. They caught the right paw across the back and the webbing, the paw already tender where her teeth had been and where the wisp's fire had found it, and the whole arm jerked out sideways with the force of it. A yelp broke out of the otter, *yip*, high and cracking in the middle. She snatched the paw back to her chest and curled it shut, the webbed fingers drawn tight together, and the blue fin along that forearm clamped flat.

Nocturne stood up straight again and let her ribs settle. The carved faces on the nearest pillar looked down at the two of them with their worn stone eyes, not caring either way.

*Slow. You were slow there.*

She looked at the otter's face. The small black eyes were wide and furious, the cheek marks twitching as she gritted her teeth, and under the fury, plain for anyone to read, was the fear she had been trying to swallow. She cradled the hurt paw against the bruised chest and did not take her eyes off Nocturne for an instant.
