# Ripples vs Nerine — three taps, three different parts

*The lakeside clearing. A submission match: the third tap-out wins, and every tap must come from a body part no
earlier tap used. The engine decided every beat (seed 8); the prose is hand-written from its results.*

---

## Part one: feeling each other out

**1.** The breeze came off the lake and laid the grass flat between them, and Ripples went in under it. She
aimed a chopping Brick Break at Nerine's right paw, the one Nerine kept forward and loose, but Nerine pulled it
back an inch and the edge of Ripples' fist came down on the meat of her upper arm instead. The shock ran down into
the fin on her forearm. Nerine hissed through her teeth and shook the arm out, already grinning.

**2.** She answered the way she always answered: fast and close. She went for Ripples' nose with her teeth, and
when Ripples jerked her head aside the bite closed on the side of her skull instead, raking down across her left
cheek and catching the scruff of her neck. Nerine yanked. Ripples was dragged half a step toward her, and then
the jaws let go and she was square again, blinking, two lines of wet heat opening along her face.

**3.** Nerine didn't let her settle. She came straight back for the right paw, missed it by a hair, and sank her
teeth into Ripples' upper arm, high, near the shoulder. She twisted into the bite with her whole small body, and
Ripples' feet went out from under her. She hit the grass flat on her back, her shoulder blades and her left hip
taking the fall. For a moment she lay there with the sky over her and the taste of blood in her mouth.

**4.** Nerine stood over her and opened her mouth, and the Water Gun came out in a hard, steady stream. Then
there was a second burst on top of the first. It hammered Ripples' face, her ear, the root of her tails; she
choked on it, sputtering, the water up her nose and down her throat. She rolled out from under it, coughing,
dug her paws into the ground and came up on the first try, streaming wet and furious.

**5.** Ripples spat out the water she had swallowed and sent it back, and more besides. The Hydro Pump hit Nerine
low and rising: right knee, right hip, then her upper back as she turned from it. It took her off her feet. She
landed on her back in the grass with a grunt, soaked through, her tails splayed.

**6.** Ripples was on her before she could rise. She meant to catch a wrist, but Nerine's right leg was the thing
in reach, and Ripples took it instead. She dragged it across a root humped out of the ground at the clearing's
edge and leaned her weight onto the shin. The knee bent backward over the hump of the root, and the hip twisted
with it. Nerine's breath went out of her in a sharp, surprised sound. She had not expected Ripples to go to the
ground with her so soon.

**7.** She didn't stay surprised. Flat on her back with her leg trapped, she curled up off the grass and bit the
nearest part of Ripples she could reach, which was her left foot, planted on the root beside the lever. Her teeth
went in deep and dragged up over the ankle, and Ripples' leg buckled. She swayed hard over the lock and somehow
didn't go down, but it was enough. Nerine wrenched the trapped leg once, hard, and it tore out of the hold. She
was up a breath later, limping on it and laughing at nothing.

**8.** Ripples let her have the laugh. She set her feet and fired the Hydro Pump again, and this time it broke over
Nerine's lower back and hip and the fin of her right forearm, which Nerine threw up too late. It spun her a
quarter-turn. Water ran off her in sheets.

**9.** Nerine turned the spin into a run. She wrapped herself in water and came in low with Aqua Jet, aiming for
the paw Ripples had been guarding. Ripples snatched the paw back, and Nerine's shoulder drove into her upper arm
instead, the same arm she had bitten, and Ripples felt it go hot and stupid from the elbow up.

**10.** Ripples answered in kind, but she aimed better. She wrapped herself in her own Aqua Jet and took Nerine at
the tail and hip, low, and she didn't stop. The water carried them both across the grass, Nerine's feet off the
ground, until the trunk of a tall pine met her back. Bark and sap and her shoulders and her right hip all hit at
once, and then Ripples' whole body crushed into her and held her there. When Ripples stepped back, Nerine slid
down the trunk and crumpled at its foot on her side, her head ringing.

**11.** Ripples didn't wait for it to stop ringing. She dropped on Nerine where she lay at the foot of the pine and
bit her left foot, then again in the same place, hard enough that something in it gave, then once into the soft
cream of her stomach before she had to come up for air. Nerine screamed at the second bite. She hauled herself up
the pine with both paws, her left foot held off the ground, and turned around with her back to the bark and her
lips pulled back.

**12.** Then she came off the tree. She flung a burst of stars at Ripples: one tore across the right paw, one
across the thigh, and one into her stomach that rocked her back onto her heels. Ripples was still bracing against
that when Nerine closed the gap with a frost-white Ice Fang. She wanted the paw again and got the fin above it,
and the cold went into the bone. Ripples shook the arm and the fin stung like a burn.

**13.** Ripples was running low on breath, and she knew it. She pushed one more Water Pulse out of herself anyway.
It caught Nerine's right knee, the knee the root lever had already bent the wrong way, along with her cheek and
the small of her back. Nerine's leg wobbled under her.

**14.** Ripples saw it wobble. She stepped in, hooked her own leg behind that knee, and shoved, and Nerine went
over backward and then onto her face as Ripples rolled her. Before she could get her paws under her, Ripples' twin
tails were wound twice around the hurt knee, cranking it one way while both of Ripples' paws took her right foot
and turned it the other. The knee was caught between them and twisted against itself.

**15.** Nerine screamed into the grass. Then, because she was Nerine, she twisted her head around and spat a Water
Pulse back over her own shoulder. It was blind and close, and it burst against Ripples' right paw, her shoulder and
her chest. Ripples flinched and didn't let go. The tails tightened. The knee went past where it wanted to go, and
Nerine's paw came up off the ground and hung there, trembling, ready to slap the grass.

She didn't let it. She pressed her face into the dirt, bared her teeth and fought the crank instead. The paw went
back down flat, the claws dug in, and she held on.

**16.** Ripples didn't hit her. She didn't need to. She just held on and kept turning, the tails cinching tighter
around the knee with every breath she drew, and her paws turning the foot a little further each time. Nerine
clawed at the grass, then at the tails, then at the grass again. Her paw lifted off the ground a second time, and
she looked at it as if it belonged to someone else. Then she slammed it back down beside her face, open, claws in
the dirt, not a tap. She sobbed once through her teeth and kept fighting it.

**17.** She found the one thing she could still swing. Face-down, one leg wound up in Ripples' tails, she whipped
her own tails around in a flail of water, and the Aqua Tail cracked across Ripples' right paw and the fin above it.
It was the paw Ripples had wrapped around the foot, the paw Nerine had been biting at all fight. Something in it
flashed white. The blow knocked Ripples clean off her and onto her back; she rolled with it, over her shoulders
and up onto her feet in one motion. But the grip was gone. Nerine dragged her leg back in to her body, and on the
second try she got the good leg under her and stood, the right one held crooked and barely touching the ground.

**18.** Ripples had come up with nothing left in her lungs. She went for the knee anyway. Her chop missed it by a
hand's width and broke across the thigh above, high toward the hip. The leg buckled a little and Nerine's weight
shifted off it, but she caught herself, square again, eyes narrowed.

**19.** Nerine went low for the bite this time, under Ripples' guard. Her teeth sank into Ripples' left hip and
dragged across the thigh and the root of her tails, and for a step Ripples was hauled along with her before the
jaws opened.

**20.** Ripples showed her teeth. She had been waiting for Nerine to come in close and low, and now Nerine was
close and low. The Swift came out of her in a hissing fan of stars. Two of them went into Nerine's cheek and chest,
but the third went where Ripples had sent everything for six beats now: into the right knee. It went in with a
sound like a stick breaking under snow. Nerine didn't fall. She stood on it, which was worse.

**21.** Nerine snapped at Ripples' foot, reconsidered halfway, and went for the paw she had already ruined. By pure
luck Ripples' left arm was in the way. The bite closed on the forearm instead and did half what it meant to do.
Then a gust came across the lake and flattened the grass around them, blew the wet fur flat against Ripples' face,
and passed on.

**22.** Ripples dragged in what air she could. Her lungs had nothing to give, but she spent it anyway on another
spray of stars, and again the worst of it went into the knee. Two more streaked across Nerine's throat and her
right upper arm. Ripples stood empty-chested afterward, swaying. She had spent everything to make that knee worse,
and it was much worse.

**23.** Nerine saw how empty she was. She gathered the last of her own breath into an Aqua Jet and went in under
Ripples' arms, low against the root of her tails. Then she lifted, the whole water-wrapped body of her driving up,
and threw her. Ripples sailed over her head and came down hard on her back in the grass, her shoulder and thigh
slapping down a heartbeat after.

**24.** For a moment neither of them did anything. Nerine stood with her weight on one leg, sides heaving,
watching. Ripples lay on her back and stared at the clouds going over, and then she rolled onto her front and
pushed. Her arms trembled, and the first time they let her down. The second time she got her feet under her and
stood straight up.

**25.** Ripples breathed out frost. The Ice Beam was meant to be a fan across Nerine's jaw and arm and that knee,
but she was tired and it came out wild: she overcommitted into it and stumbled forward off her line as it left
her. It still found the knee, and it found the left foot, which was something swollen and wrong now from the
earlier bites, and it found the upper arm she had chopped in the first exchange. Frost crept into the fur over all
three.

**26.** Nerine came in at the stumble. She went for Ripples' leg first and changed her mind for the paw, the right
paw that she'd bitten and tail-slammed and frozen, and this time she got it cleanly and bit down until something
inside it crunched. Ripples' whole body flinched away from the pain. Nerine used the flinch, heaved, and threw her
down onto her back in the grass by the paw she was holding, then let it go.

**27.** Nerine dropped beside her before she could roll away. She caught the ruined right paw by the wrist and
folded it up and back. Then she threaded her own arm under Ripples' elbow from the other side and locked her paws
together over her wrist, and Ripples' shoulder was caught at the end of the lever. Nerine leaned back. The shoulder
turned in its socket, and everything below it, every bitten and frozen and broken thing in that arm, was dragged
around with it.

**28.** Nerine didn't need to do anything else. She just breathed in and leaned. Ripples arched up off the grass,
braced and strained against her, her heels digging trenches, and none of it moved the lock. Her left paw came up
on its own. It hovered over the grass and shook. She looked at it. Then she closed it into a fist and pressed it
down into the dirt beside her instead, not flat, and dragged in a breath through her teeth.

**29.** She hit back with the only arm she had. Her left paw came up out of the grass in a Brick Break aimed at
Nerine's own right paw, the one locked over her wrist, and it chopped down onto the fin of Nerine's forearm with a
crack. It hurt Nerine and it didn't loosen anything. Nerine leaned harder. The shoulder made a sound. Ripples
screamed and her heels skidded in the grass, and still she didn't tap.

**30.** Nerine kept the lever on with one arm and spat a Water Gun into Ripples' face with the rest of her. It
spattered across her ear, her other fin, and the upper arm that was locked in the hold, and Ripples had nothing
free to cover up with. Ripples twisted under it and the lock loosened a fraction; Nerine felt it give and snapped
her teeth into the fin on Ripples' forearm to punish it. Ripples' left paw went flat on the grass. It lifted. It
went flat again. It didn't slap.

**31.** Then Ripples opened her mouth and breathed winter. The Ice Beam went straight up into Nerine at
point-blank: across her thigh, her ear, her neck, the cold held on her for three long pulses. Nerine's grip
didn't break from the cold, but her body flinched back from it, and when it did Ripples shoved her bodily off and
tore the arm free. She lay there for a moment, cradling it. Then she tried to rise, failed, tried again, failed,
and on the third try came up on her knees and then her feet, the right arm hanging.

**32.** Nerine came off the grass with her tails already moving. The Aqua Tail cracked across Ripples' right cheek
and the side of her head and turned her face to the left. Her legs went loose; she staggered back two steps and
nearly sat down, but she didn't. She stayed on her feet, blinking water out of her eyes.

**33.** Nerine had nothing left behind the next one. She threw a Brick Break at Ripples' ear and put everything she
had into it, too much, and it was tired and wide and came down on her muzzle instead. Ripples' head rocked back.
Nerine stumbled forward past her, off balance, her bad leg nearly folding under her.

**34.** Ripples turned with her and breathed frost into her face as she went by. The Ice Beam held for two pulses,
across Nerine's muzzle, her upper back, and the left knee, the good one, which until now nobody had touched.
Nerine flinched from that one more than from the cold.

**35.** She came back for the paw. She seized Ripples' right wrist with both paws and tried to fold it back on
itself, standing, but the small joint was slick and Ripples stiffened it against her, and it slid out of her grip
before it locked. Nerine snarled.

**36.** Ripples answered with her whole body. She dropped and spun, her tails sweeping Nerine's good leg out, and as
Nerine went down face-first Ripples went down with her and tangled both her legs around the bad one. She trapped
the heel in the crook of her arm and turned it. The twist ran up through the ankle into the knee, and the knee was
already the most broken thing in the clearing.

**37.** Ripples just held it. She lay tangled around the leg with the heel locked in her elbow and leaned back a
little further each breath. Nerine clawed at the grass and at Ripples' legs and at the grass again, and her claws
found a seam in the tangle and pried. For a moment the grip went slack and the heel turned back toward where it
belonged. Ripples felt it go and clamped down again, but not as tight.

**38.** Nerine twisted onto her side as far as the hold would let her and sprayed water back down the length of her
own body at whatever she could hit: Ripples' upper arm, her thigh, the fin of her good arm. It was weak and wild and
it made Ripples blink. It didn't make her let go. The heel turned. Nerine pried at it with both paws, shrieking,
and her own paw rose and slapped the grass beside the heel and then stopped, a finger's breadth from the second
slap. She held it there and shook her head and screamed "*No*" into the ground.

**39.** Ripples breathed and leaned. The knee was the color of a storm under the fur. Nerine threw her weight one way
and then the other, and for a second time the grip loosened under her when she found the angle, and for a second
time it didn't open. She hung there in it, gasping, every muscle in her body shaking, and didn't tap.

**40.** She bit instead. She twisted up out of the grass as far as her spine would go and sank her teeth into
whatever was in reach, which was the top of Ripples' left shoulder, and dragged them along her neck and over her
throat. Ripples screamed and was dragged toward her by it. Her legs came loose for an instant, and in that instant
Nerine wrenched the leg out with one hard, ugly heave. She tried to get up and the knee dropped her, and the drop
jarred something; she tried again and her back hit the grass. On the third try she found her feet and stood
there on one leg, panting, her muzzle wet with someone else's blood.

**41.** Ripples came up already shooting. The Water Pulse was meant for Nerine's legs and back. But Nerine met it
with a fistful of Swift, and the stars went through the water and broke it apart and blew the spray back into
Ripples' own face. It burst across both her cheeks and the side of her head, and she reeled, shaking her ears.

**42.** It made her angry, and angry made her precise. She came in under Nerine's guard with her teeth frosted and
went for the nose, missed it, and caught the whole muzzle instead. She caught it at exactly the right angle and the
right moment, the jaws closing like a trap. The cold drove through Nerine's muzzle into her cheek. Nerine reeled
back from it with frost in her whiskers, chilled through, her breath coming out white.

**43.** Ripples went after the next thing. The knee was finished; she could see it in the way Nerine stood, every
ounce of her weight on the other leg. So Ripples went for the other leg. Her chop came down at the left foot, slid
off it, and landed on the hip above. The good leg buckled a little. It held.

**44.** Nerine lunged at her with Ice Fang, at the shoulder, and Ripples just wasn't there. She slipped aside, and
the frozen jaws snapped shut on nothing.

**45.** Nerine didn't stop. She turned with the miss and sprayed a Water Gun across Ripples' neck and paw and hip,
and some of it went straight into Ripples' open mouth. Ripples doubled over and choked, coughing, the water
coming back up out of her nose.

**46.** She came up out of the cough swinging. Her Brick Break went low for the left foot again, slid off it again,
and chopped into the thigh above it and the hip. The leg buckled. Nerine's weight had nowhere left to go, but she
stood. She stood longer than a blow like that should have let her.

**47.** Ripples let her stand. She backed off two steps and just breathed, still coughing up lake water, her
ruined paw held against her chest. Nerine stood on one leg and watched her and breathed too.

---

## Part two: the first tap

**48.** It was Nerine who moved first. She hopped in on the good leg, hooked it behind Ripples' knee and pulled,
and Ripples went down on her face with Nerine on her back. Before she could get her good arm under her, Nerine had
the ruined paw in both of hers. She pinned Ripples' elbow against her own ribs and folded the paw back on itself at
the wrist. It had nowhere to go. It went anyway.

**49.** Ripples screamed into the grass, then twisted her head round and spat a fan of Swift back over her shoulder.
The stars found Nerine's left foot, the one the bites had swollen up, and her paw and her upper arm, and the foot
blow rocked her sideways. It was the kind of blow that would have broken any grip of hers five minutes earlier.
Now it didn't. She held on, and something came up through her instead: her eyes went wide and bright and her
breathing changed. Adrenaline flooded her, and the pain fell away behind it like a door closing.

**50.** She used it. With one paw still folding Ripples' wrist, she flung Swift down into the back of Ripples'
head point-blank, into her cheek and her thigh, and the blow to her cheek knocked Ripples' face sideways into the
grass and held it there. Ripples could not turn it any further. She couldn't cover up; both her arms were caught
under her. The wrist folded another fraction. Ripples' left paw hit the grass once, flat, and then clawed instead,
dragging up a fistful of grass and dirt. Not a tap. Not yet.

**51.** She had one more thing in her. She reached back blind with the good paw and chopped at the left foot that
was planted by her hip, and she found it. Brick Break came down on the swollen foot with everything she had, and
Nerine's whole body jerked. But the adrenaline held her together, and she stayed on Ripples' back with the wrist
in her paws. She leaned. Ripples' scream went up into the trees and the birds went up after it.

**52.** Then Nerine leaned down to Ripples' ear and feinted with her teeth, a snap that Ripples read and ignored.
She bit the ear for real a moment later, frosted, the cold driving into the side of Ripples' head, and as Ripples
jerked away from it the wrist turned the last fraction it had.

Ripples tapped.

Her left paw slapped the grass, once, twice, three times, fast, frantic. Nerine let go at once and rolled off her
and away. Ripples lay where she was for a moment with her right paw curled up against her, the fingers of it not
working. Then she tried to rise. Her knees went from under her and she fell back. She tried again, and fell again,
and the third time she only got as far as sitting up, hunched, her ruined paw in her lap. Something else rose in
her instead of her body: the same thing Nerine had found a few breaths before. Her own adrenaline came up in a
wave, and her black eyes went flat and cold.

*One tap to Nerine. Ripples' right paw can't count for another.*

**53.** Nerine had stepped back to let her get up, as the match required, and that was a mistake. Ripples didn't
get up. She opened her mouth where she sat and the Hydro Pump came out of her with everything the surge had given
her. It took Nerine in the left foot, the swollen foot, and something in it went for good. Then it climbed to her
ear and her nose and knocked her flat on her back in the grass. Ripples got up after that, slowly, on the third
try, putting weight on her ruined paw by mistake and nearly falling again.

**54.** Then she walked over to where Nerine lay and knelt on the grass by her legs, and she wound her twin tails
around the right knee for the second time that afternoon. She took the right foot in both paws, and she started
to turn.

**55.** Nerine looked up at her, at the tails, at the knee that had stopped answering her, and something in her
face went very calm. She had nothing left to save anything for. She came up off her back and threw everything she
had into one Brick Break, one last blow, and it came down on Ripples' right upper arm with a sound like an axe
going into wet wood. Then it carried on through the fin and into her chest. It rocked Ripples off her line.
Somehow her tails stayed on the knee. Ripples looked at Nerine with something like respect in her eyes and
thought: *careful. She has one more in her.* Nerine fell back. Her right leg lay there in the tails, and when she
told it to kick, nothing happened.

**56.** Ripples had one more in her too. She knelt there with the tails locked and her own arm hanging and poured
everything that was left into a burst of Swift at point-blank. It went into the swollen left foot, into Nerine's
head, across her frost-bitten muzzle. It drove her below the point where anyone sensible would stop, and then
some way past it. The tails turned. The knee was crushed between them.

Nerine's eyes rolled up white for a moment. Then they came back, and she bared her teeth up at the sky. *Not like
this.* Not on her back in the grass with nothing left in her, not to a Buizel she had been beating ten beats ago.
She dug her claws into the earth and held on.

**57.** Nerine didn't fight it this time. She lay on her back with the tails wound around her knee and her eyes on
the sky, breathing in shallow sips, and let it hurt. Ripples cranked. Something in the knee that had been holding
on stopped. Nerine's mouth opened and nothing came out of it, and her paw didn't move from the grass.

**58.** Ripples leaned down to finish it and bit at her face with frozen teeth. She was too tired to aim and it went
in sloppy, along Nerine's cheek and muzzle and down onto her throat, and she nearly toppled over her own bite. The
cold went into Nerine's face. The crank went on. Nerine still didn't tap. She was far past the place where
anything should be holding her together, and she didn't tap.

**59.** Then she bit back. She curled up off the ground, under the tails, and her frozen fangs found the side of
Ripples' head and her cheek. Ripples was already off balance from her own bite, and Nerine heaved and flung her
off. Ripples went over backward across the grass, rolled across the root-knotted ground beyond it on her side, and
stopped in the open. She lay there and curled up around her hurt arm, shaking. Nerine tried to get up and couldn't.
She tried again and couldn't. The third time she got as far as sitting up, propped on her paws, staring at Ripples
across the grass. *Careful,* she thought. *She's not done either.*

**60.** Ripples dragged herself to Nerine on her belly. She didn't get up; she couldn't. She came at her from the
side, low, caught Nerine's right arm where it braced on the grass and trapped it straight under her own armpit.
Then she wrenched up against the elbow and bent Nerine forward over it where she sat. Nerine's whole body was
shaking, and it wasn't from the cold.

**61.** Nerine flung Swift at her from inches away. It was wild, all her weight thrown behind it so that she nearly
toppled out of the hold, but it raked across Ripples' fin, her back, the paw she still had. It took Ripples to the
edge of everything she had left. Ripples didn't let go. She looked at Nerine bent double over her own arm and
thought, very clearly: *she's done. Finish it.*

**62.** She leaned on the elbow. Nerine twisted against it and the arm stayed straight. Her sitting body sagged
forward over the lock, and Ripples felt it start to go and held it where it was.

---

## Part three: the scramble

**63.** Then Nerine did something neither of them had planned. She let her body fall the way the armbar was bending
it, forward and across Ripples, and as she fell she caught Ripples' right arm, the wrecked one, and dragged it
across her own hips and threw her weight back against the elbow. For a moment they were knotted together, each
with the other's arm wrenched straight. Ripples screamed. Nerine screamed louder.

Nerine tapped. Her free paw slapped Ripples' back, twice, three times, and Ripples let go of her arm. But Nerine
did not let go of Ripples'.

*One tap to Ripples. Nerine's right arm can't count for another. One tap each.*

Ripples hung in the lock with her own health past empty, and something in her flatly refused to be put down by
it. Her eyes stayed open. Nerine, past empty too, felt the grey creep in at the edges of the world and held on.

**64.** Ripples tried to wind her free arm round Nerine's for a lock of her own and couldn't. She had nothing to do
it with and nowhere to do it from, her arm pinned straight across Nerine's hips. All she could do was lie there
and breathe and feel the elbow being bent the wrong way. She didn't tap. She was running on nothing.

**65.** So she used what she had. Nerine was half-sitting over her, her back to Ripples' face, hauling on the arm.
Ripples got her free paw up around Nerine's head from behind and hooked it under her chin. Then she wound her tails
up around her own wrist to lock it there and hauled back. Nerine's head came back, and her jaw with it, and her
spine bent over Ripples' knee where it was driven into her back. Now they were both caught. Neither of them would
let go.

**66.** Nerine had one paw free and used it to spray water point-blank into Ripples' face and shoulder and the fin
of the arm she was breaking. Ripples' body jerked in the lock and the lock took it. The elbow went. At the same
moment Ripples' paw and tails hauled Nerine's chin the last inch back, and Nerine's back gave a long, ugly creak.

They tapped together. Two paws slapped the grass at the same moment, Ripples' left and Nerine's right, and both
grips opened.

*Two taps each. One more ends it. Neither of them has a right arm that counts any more, and Ripples has lost her
right paw, and Nerine her back.*

They rolled apart and lay on the grass a body's length from each other, and neither of them could get up. They
tried three times each and fell back three times each.

**67.** They lay there. Ripples didn't even get as far as sitting. Nerine tried to sit and her sight went grey at
the edges and the sounds of the clearing went far away, and she put her face down in the grass until it came
back. When it did, she looked across at Ripples lying limp on her back, not even trying to rise, and thought:
*she's done. Get up. Finish it.*

---

## Part four: the last part that counts

**68.** Ripples made it to Nerine on her knees and elbows. Nerine was sitting up again, swaying, and Ripples came
up behind her and got her good forearm across Nerine's throat and her other arm, the dead one, behind Nerine's
head. She tried to press it forward with that paw, and the paw didn't answer. It hung off the end of her arm like
something tied there. So she used her chest instead, pressed hard into Nerine's back, and squeezed with the one
arm she had.

**69.** Nerine reached back over her own shoulder and flung stars into Ripples' face, and they burst across her ear
and her fin and the root of her tails. Then she did the most unlikely thing in the fight. She drove her head back
into Ripples' face, shoved with everything she had, and Ripples' grip slid off her throat. Then they both tried
to stand. Nerine fell on her ruined knee and came up again on the third try. Ripples fell on her ruined paw and
came up again on the third try, too. They were on their feet facing each other across a few lengths of trampled
grass, swaying, both far past empty.

**70.** Ripples sent a Water Pulse into Nerine's knee and thigh and neck. She was so tired she nearly went over
with it, and some of it went into Nerine's open mouth, and Nerine choked and sputtered on her feet. Neither of them
fell. Their bodies were telling them both to lie down, and neither of them did.

**71.** Ripples set her feet. She took a breath that rattled, and she threw a fan of Swift at Nerine with every
scrap of aim she had left. One star went into the right knee, into the exact point where the bone was already
broken. Nerine doubled over and staggered backward, several steps, nearly going down along the line of it. She was
shaking all over. Ripples looked at her and thought: *she's done.*

**72.** Nerine didn't think she was done. She spat a Water Gun across the gap, and Ripples met it with a Hydro Pump,
and the bigger water swallowed the smaller and drove it back down Nerine's throat and across her face and her ear.
Nerine stood there with her muzzle running and her eyes unfocused. Her body was overruling her now. She went on
standing anyway.

**73.** Ripples threw one more fan of stars into the knee, and her twin tails, and her chest. Nerine staggered back
two steps, three, and then her body simply stopped. Her legs folded and she went down on her side in the grass,
not from the blow, just from having nothing left. Across from her, the grey came in at the edges of Ripples' sight,
too. Nerine lay on her side looking at her through it. *Careful,* she thought. *She's got one more.*

**74.** She did, and so did Nerine. From the ground, Nerine chopped at Ripples' right arm as she came near, one
chop that went down through the fin into the wrecked paw and up into the upper arm, all of it already broken. It
staggered Ripples two steps to her left. Then Ripples' body did the same thing Nerine's had: her legs simply gave,
and she went down flat on her back beside her.

**75.** There was a root humped up out of the grass beside them, the same kind of root this had started on. Ripples
saw it. She rolled toward Nerine, got hold of the wrecked right leg, and dragged it across the root. Then she laid
her whole weight onto the shin. The knee bent backward over the hump. Nerine lay on her side and shook.

**76.** Nerine wasn't finished. She twisted, got hold of Ripples' left foot and trapped it under her arm, and clamped
her knees on the leg above. She wrenched it back and round, a lock of her own, so that the two of them lay in the
grass each holding the other's leg. She didn't tap. Something came up in Ripples then: a second wind, from nowhere,
from far below anywhere she should have been able to find one. Her eyes cleared.

**77.** Ripples used it to bite. Still lying on the lever, she bent down and buried her teeth in Nerine's right knee,
just above the root, and the bite went through everything that was left. Nerine's whole body convulsed and her grip
on Ripples' foot sprang open. Ripples' weight stayed on the lever. Nerine's knee was jarred by nothing more than
her own heartbeat, and she cried out at that, too. Then her sight went grey.

**78.** She hit Ripples one more time. With the last of everything, she chopped down onto the fin of the arm that
was holding her leg, and drove Ripples' wrist into the grass, and the fin went numb under the blow. Ripples didn't
let go.

Then Nerine's paw came up and slapped the ground. Once, then again and again, harder than it needed to.

Ripples felt it and let go.

*Ripples wins, three taps to two: Nerine's right arm, her back, and her right knee.*

---

For a long time neither of them moved. Ripples lay on her back in the grass with her ruined arm across her chest,
and when she tried to sit up her body said no, and she let it. Somewhere out on the lake a fish rose, and the
rings spread out across the bright water and lapped at the sand. Nerine lay on her side with her leg still stretched
across the root where Ripples had left it. She was awake and she knew exactly what had happened, and she felt all
of it.

After a while Nerine turned her head toward Ripples. Neither of them said anything. The birds came back to the trees
one at a time.
