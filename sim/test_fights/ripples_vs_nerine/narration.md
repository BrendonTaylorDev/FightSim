# Ripples vs Nerine — the lakeside clearing

Test fight 5 (build 206). Scene 15, knockouts only, no pins, default scale. Played beat by beat through
`sim/harness.py` with a scripted director (`plan.py`, `drive.py`), and narrated by hand from the engine's notes
(`engine_log.txt` holds the mechanics of every beat).

Styles for this fight: **Nerine** fights as a brawler, quick to close, grab, shove and throw, using the trees, the
trunk and the sand. **Ripples** is patient and stubborn. She keeps her distance, waits for the rush, answers it, and
binds whatever she can reach with her tails.

---

## Beat 1 — Quick Attack

Nerine didn't circle at all.

Ripples had expected at least that much: a lap of the grass, a feint, a look. She had her weight set low and her
tails turning slow behind her, ready to read whatever came. What came was a dark-red blur off the far side of the
clearing. The warm smell of pine resin drifted across the gap between them, and the river-bred Buizel was already
gathered low on her toes, fins flared wide on both forearms. She was eager in the hard, bright way a fighter is when
she has been waiting all day for this.

*She's going to make me come to her. No.*

Nerine launched. Three strides to build it and the fourth was all thrust, grass tearing under her webbed feet. At the
last instant she dropped her shoulder, so the point of it went in first.

It took Ripples square in the chest with a dull, heavy thud. The force ran through her front and rang her right arm
where it was folded across her body. Her chest caved round the blow and her shoulders rounded over it. The air went
out of her in one hard push and came back with a burn in it. A tiny yip escaped her, *Yp*, half surprise. Her right
arm jerked back and stayed tucked against her side a moment, buzzing as though the blow had plucked a string
between her ribs and her elbow.

Her legs braced, every muscle in her thighs bunching, and she didn't go back a step. She blinked hard once. Her lip
curled off her teeth, more anger than hurt.

*Fine. You're fast. Fast isn't everything.*

Nerine skipped back out of reach on the balls of her feet, grinning, the black cheek marks twitching. *One–nothing.*

## Beat 2 — Water Gun

Ripples gave ground on purpose: one step back, then another, letting Nerine see her retreat. She chittered low in
her throat, *Tch-tch*. Her right arm was still tight against her side.

When Nerine came forward again, eager, Ripples puffed her bare throat, set her feet and spat.

The jet hit Nerine full in the face with a gushing thud of water on fur. Her head snapped back, eyes streaming. The
spray drove up her muzzle and she snorted it out in a sneeze, tossing her head. The rest of it splashed across her
chest. An offended little bark burst out of her, *Hah!*

But it was water, and Nerine was a Buizel. The jet spent itself against her like rain off a roof: it soaked her and
stung and did very little else. Her fur ran dark and heavy. Water dripped from her chin, beaded on her whiskers and
ran in rivulets down the cream of her throat. She shook her head hard, flinging droplets that caught the sun.

Ripples watched it fall short and filed that away. *Water's nothing to her. Same as me. All right.*

Nerine wiped her eyes with the back of one wrist and laughed through her nose. *That's it? That's what you've got?*

## Beat 3 — The grab, and the flurry

She came in through the next spray before Ripples could think of anything else to use. She ducked under it, kept
low, and closed both webbed paws on Ripples' right upper arm.

It wasn't a crushing grip; it barely hurt. It was a handle. Ripples felt the paws fasten and knew at once what they
were for. Nerine's face was suddenly right there, a hand's width away, wet fur and bared teeth and quick hot breath.
Neither of them had room to slip anything now.

Ripples shoved at the grip once, hard, ears flattening. It didn't move.

Nerine didn't give her a second try. Her free paw came down in short chopping blows, no wind-up, nothing to see
coming, each one a dull heavy thud at no distance at all.

The first went into Ripples' chest, on the spot the Quick Attack had already found. Her chest caved round it, the
shoulders rounding, and a tiny yip broke out of her. This one didn't fade the way the first had. A soreness settled
into her ribs and stayed. The second chop glanced up onto her right shoulder; her shoulders hunched round it with a
startled *Prrt*. The third came down on the right arm Nerine was holding. The pressure built against the muscle,
the fur dented, and the limb went numb for an instant and came back throbbing. The jolt jerked her whole body inside
the hold. The grip took it and kept her exactly where she was.

Ripples twisted then, turning her shoulder into the next chop so it had nowhere clean to land. That spoiled the
flurry. Nerine's grip didn't loosen.

*Let go of my arm.* Her bark came out offended and small: *Hah.*

Nerine only grinned wider.

## Beat 4 — Thrown into the oak

She swung her.

Ripples felt the turn begin through the arm Nerine held. Her feet left the grass and the clearing spun past: sky,
pines, the glitter of the lake. For a moment there was nothing under her at all. Then the grip let go, torn off her
arm as she went.

The old oak met her back-first.

Her upper back hit the bark, then the small of her back, then her head knocked against the trunk. The rough wood
scraped her fur against the grain. Her shoulders hunched up round the impact and an indignant chitter was jolted out
of her. Her lower back took it with a quick sharp breath through the nose, and her face screwed tight as her skull
met the bark.

She slid off it and dropped into the roots at its foot. Her left hip landed on a humped root, and the small of her
back came down across another. Her leg jerked out sideways with a startled *Pip*. The tumble ended there. She didn't
roll through it. She lay on her back among the roots, face up to the leaves, her chest and belly open.

None of it hurt badly. It all hurt a little, everywhere, and the leaves above her swam once and steadied.

*Get up. No — breathe first. Then get up.*

She lay there and breathed, watching Nerine shake her paws out on the grass.

## Beat 5 — The dropping knee

Nerine didn't let her finish breathing.

Ripples saw her come and knew what it was a heartbeat too late. Nerine was in the air, one knee leading, coming down
at her middle. Ripples got her arms halfway across her belly. It made no difference.

The knee drove into her stomach with the crack of a hard hit. There was nowhere to roll with it: the ground was
under her and the root-knotted earth took the blow from below as the knee took it from above. The small of her back
was pressed down into a root. Her eyes flew wide and the pupils shrank. Her back tried to round and fold over the
blow and couldn't; she could only curl her head and knees up round it. The muscle of her belly was crushed flat and
sprang back, and a wave of sickness rose from deep in her gut. Her legs felt very far away. A cross little huff,
*Hff*, was all the air she had for a sound. The blow shunted her a hand's width along the ground.

It left a deep soreness behind, a bruise she could feel spreading under the fur.

Nerine bounced back up off her, grinning.

Ripples rolled over onto her side and planted her paws in the roots. She made it to her knees and stayed there,
head down, one breath, two, a webbed paw spread flat against her stomach. She swallowed. Then she pushed the rest of
the way up and stood. She looked down at her own middle, pressed it once with her fingers and winced.

*That one's going to stay. Every bend, I'll feel it.*

Nerine was already shaking the last of the water out of her fur, dry and fluffed again. The soaking was gone.

## Beat 6 — Ice Fang on the knee

Ripples waited.

Nerine came again, of course. She stepped in close and planted her left leg for a shove, putting her whole weight
on it.

Ripples dropped under the shove and bit the knee.

Frost bloomed white on her teeth as they closed. They sank in, the skin dragged taut round them, and she pulled the
knee toward her with a grinding squeak of teeth on hide. The joint jolted sideways. The cold ran down into Nerine's
foot and up into her hip in the same instant. Nerine barked, *Bwah*, and her leg shook when she set it down. She
shifted her weight off the foot for a step and flicked the hip out as if to shake the sting off.

But it was ice, and Nerine was a water-bred Buizel like her. The frost crusted on her fur and spent itself there. It
did less than it looked like it should, and they both knew it. Still, what got in stayed in: a shiver ran up
Nerine's body, frost glittered in the orange fur of her thigh, and her steps for the next while came stiff and slow.

Ripples let go and backed off, chin tucked, throat covered.

*Slower now. Good.*

## Beat 7 — A breath

Neither of them pressed.

Ripples backed off a step, shook her head to clear the ring the oak had put in it, and watched. She kept her
weight off her stomach, standing a little bent round it. Her tails turned slowly behind her, cream tips
circling.

Nerine stamped her chilled leg against the grass, once, twice, as if she could stamp the cold out of it. Her tails
lashed. Somewhere out on the lake a fish rose, and the ring of it spread across the bright water.

*Come on, then,* Ripples thought. *Come to me again.*

## Beat 8 — Aqua Jet into the pine

She did.

Nerine came off the mark the way she had at the start, low and straight. This time Ripples was already moving to
meet her. Water burst round her body in a hissing sheath and she drove in under Nerine's rush, low and level.

They met chest to chest with a heavy slap of water. The water itself did little to her. It was water against
water; it soaked and stung and barely bit. But the charge didn't stop. Ripples kept driving, her legs and the spin of
her tails pushing her on, and Nerine went backward inside the water with her. The sheath pressed and scoured at her
chest, her stomach, her left arm and right shoulder the whole way across the clearing. Her feet scrabbled for the
grass and found nothing.

The tall pine stopped them.

Nerine's back hit it first, then her left shoulder; her right thigh banged against a root flare. A chirrup of
surprise was jolted out of her, *Prrt*. Then the whole weight of the charge crushed into her front, pinning her
between Ripples and the trunk. Her head snapped back against the bark. The breath and a spray of spit went out of
her, and she was squeezed through the whole of her middle. She tried to keep the squeal behind her teeth and it
came out as a choked *Eek*, no air behind it.

Ripples let the water go and stepped back.

Nerine slid down the pine. Her legs folded and she crumpled at its foot, onto her side. She was soaked again and
dripping, her fur flattened dark, and sticky resin had pulled at the fur of her back where it met the bark.

Ripples stood over her for a moment, breathing hard. The bruise in her stomach was a hot weight with every breath.
She pressed a paw to it, looked down at it, and pulled the paw away. It would bend, if she made it. It wouldn't like
it.

*Don't stand over her. She's waiting for that.*

She stepped back.

## Beat 9 — Up the bark

Nerine spat lake water into the pine needles at the foot of the trunk. She rolled onto her front, dug her claws into
the bark and climbed her own body back up it, glaring the whole way. Her chest heaved. By the time she was standing
her fur was spiky with water and resin, and the last of the reeling had cleared from her eyes.

She bared her teeth at Ripples. *Lucky.*

Ripples didn't think it had been luck.

## Beat 10 — Scruff and bite

Nerine shoved off the trunk and came in so fast that Ripples' guard was still where she had left it.

A paw fisted itself in the scruff of Ripples' neck: a grab, not a squeeze, the fur smoothed down hard under the
fingers. Ripples huffed and planted her feet. Nerine had her again, face to face. Then Nerine hauled her head down
and bit.

She meant it for the left shoulder. Ripples turned her chin in at the last instant, and the teeth found her chest
instead, the hurt chest, the one already bruised twice.

The jaws closed with a grinding squeak of teeth on hide, fur bunched and skin stretched tight round every tooth.
Held by the scruff, Ripples had nothing free to cover it with and no room to give. Her ribs flexed in under the bite
and sprang back. The force ran up into her neck. Her whole body rocked with it, and a pained squeal was cut off
short, *Eek—*. A paw pressed itself to her chest on its own. The jaws wrenched at her and she went with them as far
as the scruff-hold let her, and no further.

She flinched. Her head jerked away from the teeth by itself and wouldn't come back for a moment. She knew she had
frozen and hated every heartbeat of it.

Nerine saw the flinch and grinned round the mouthful.

When the teeth let go, the ache stayed behind in the exact shape of the bite. Ripples took stock of it the only way
she could, with a shallow breath that stopped short of the top. It caught there, and she let it out slowly.
*Hurting now. Really hurting. She keeps finding the same place.*

Nerine watched the breath catch. She watched how Ripples breathed shallow, not letting her chest fill, and the
hitch at the top of each breath that she hid at once.

*There it is,* Nerine thought. *That's the one.*

## Beat 11 — Slammed down on the roots

Nerine still had the scruff. She reached down with the other paw, caught Ripples' arm, and lifted.

Ripples' feet left the ground. For one long moment her full weight hung between Nerine's paws. Then Nerine brought
her straight down, back-first, onto the roots at their feet.

Her upper back hit first, her shoulders hunching up round it with a tiny yip. The small of her back came down across
a root and arched away from it. A chitter went up into something sharper, and the place stiffened and stayed stiff.
Her head hit last, against the knotted ground, hard enough to screw her face up tight. A sharp huff came out
through her teeth, *Fff*. Her ears rang.

The grip on her scruff tore loose as she landed. Then she was on her back again, alone, looking up at Nerine
against the leaves.

She lay there with her breath coming short and quick. Her ribs worked under the fur, and her ears flicked back each
time she moved her chest.

The flinch was gone at least. Whatever had frozen her had let go, and she could make her body answer again. Nerine
saw her come back, saw the black eyes sharpen, and stepped in.

Her stomach still throbbed under it all, from the knee two beats ago. Ripples pressed her paw to it without meaning
to. *That one's not going anywhere.*

## Beat 12 — Tail Bind from the ground

Nerine stood too close.

Both of Ripples' tails whipped up off the ground and round Nerine's right leg, one over the knee and one round the
thigh. They cinched and wrenched.

It was a squeeze, not a strike. The muscle pressed flat under the coils and bulged out round their edges. Nerine
tested the weight with a shove, then another, breath steady. *Huh.*

Ripples tried to get up with Nerine's leg still in her tails. She planted her paws in the roots and pushed. Halfway
up, her battered back gave out and she dropped hard onto her upper back with an angry huff. Her second try was
slower and uglier, and it worked. She came up onto her feet, unsteady and winded, her sides heaving. Her tails were
wound round Nerine's leg the whole time, and she never let them slip.

They stood almost hip to hip. Nerine's right leg was caught behind her, the knee going numb and tingling under the
coil.

Nerine looked her over. The ears flicked back each time Ripples moved, and her breath was short and quick, the ribs
working under the fur. *She's hurt. She's hurt and she's holding on anyway.*

## Beat 13 — The struggle starts

Nerine hopped. She yanked. She chopped down at the coils with the edge of her paw, and they only sank deeper. The
fur and flesh squeezed up round them in a ridge.

The knee was going numb under them, weak and slow to answer. She flexed her shoulders to see what would give. Nothing
did. She grunted and braced and tried another angle, then another, her breath coming faster. Her tails lashed
behind her, and an angry whistle hissed out through her teeth.

Ripples didn't do anything at all. She just held on, chin tucked, and let Nerine fight the coil she was standing on.
Every time she breathed in, her chest stopped short. She tested it with a slightly deeper breath, and the bite-ache
flared and she let it go.

*Hurting. Fine. Hurting doesn't make me let go.*

## Beat 14 — Water Gun at point-blank, and the wrench

With Nerine's leg caught, Ripples turned her face up and spat the hardest jet she had straight into it.

Nerine got a forearm halfway up. It was too late, and the bind on her leg left her no step to take out of the way.
The water caught her head, her throat and her chest at once with a wet crack and a patter of falling drops. Her face
screwed up and her head ducked. The jet pressed into her throat across its whole width, the fur pushed flat and
rippling outward. Her breath stopped mid-draw and came back crushed. A squeak was strangled into nothing.

It was water, and most of it ran off her. She was soaked a third time, dripping and heavy, and it barely hurt. The
part that did hurt was in her chest: a wheeze snagged at the top of each breath after.

All the while, the tails tightened on her knee and thigh. The ache under them came up through the numbness, and
both places started to throb.

That did it. Nerine stopped testing. She kicked. She clawed at the coils. She bucked her whole body against the
leg, ears pinned flat, the sounds coming out of her rougher and louder. Then she threw everything into one hard
wrench, and her leg came free.

Ripples was left holding nothing.

## Beat 15 — The shoulder barge and the fallen trunk

Nerine shook her leg out once and went straight back in, shoulder down.

The barge hit Ripples in the chest. It was the fourth time something had hit her there, and it hurt the most by far.
It went into a place that was already hurting, already soft. The blow rocked her whole body, and the jolt rang down
into her right arm. A squeak was bitten off short, *Ik*, and something in her chest went from hurting to much, much
worse.

Then the charge didn't stop. Nerine kept driving, feet scrabbling in the grass, all the way across the clearing,
until the fallen trunk met the backs of Ripples' legs and her back.

Her upper back slammed into the mossy wood. A groan was dragged up out of her, her shoulders hunching round it. Her
left shoulder took the trunk too, and her right thigh banged against the log. Then Nerine's full weight crushed into
her front and pinned her there.

That was the moment it became too much.

The pain in her chest stopped being something she could hold. She heard herself make the sound before she felt
herself make it: a thin squeal forced through her clenched teeth, *Eeeh*. It wasn't a scream. She still had too much
in her to scream. But it was a sound she had never meant to let out. Both paws clamped over her chest. Her eyes
squeezed shut, and for a long, slow moment there was nothing in her head but that one place: hot, swollen, wrong.
Every breath tried to fill her chest and was stopped halfway by the pain, and she had to sip the air.

*Not there. Not again. Not there.*

Nerine stepped back.

Ripples slid down the moss and crumpled at the foot of the trunk, onto her side. She curled round her chest with a
paw clamped to it and her knees drawn up, each breath a short, hitching sip. She didn't try to get up. She couldn't
have.

Nerine stood over her and looked. She saw a wince Ripples could no longer hide, every time she moved. She saw her
mouth hanging a little open, because she breathed through it now. And she saw the way her chest wouldn't fill.

Nerine's own breath was coming hard. Her right knee ached from the bind, and she was glad Ripples couldn't see it.
*Done. Almost done.* She wasn't sure she believed it.

## Beat 16 — The pummel against the trunk

Nerine dropped to one knee over her and started chopping.

Ripples was on her side with nowhere to roll. The ground was under her and the trunk was behind her, and her arms
were already wrapped round her chest. She got one elbow out to cover her belly and it made no difference.

The first chop went into her stomach, onto the bruise the knee had left. It drove her down into the grass, and her
whole body jerked under it. A real cry tore out of her, a whimpering chitter that went up high, *Ki-ii*. The second
came down on the same place before the first had finished. This time she screamed. It wasn't long or loud, but she
couldn't help it. She curled round her middle and shook, her breath coming in ragged pulls.

The third chop was lighter, on her thigh. She barely felt it under the rest. The fourth found her left knee, the
fifth found the knee again, and the sixth found it a third time. Each one landed on a leg that was still flinching
from the last, until the knee buckled in against her body and stayed there. The seventh went into the small of her
back. She twisted away from it and couldn't reach far enough to get it clear.

Then, somehow, she hit back. Her right paw came out from under her and caught Nerine high on the left arm. It was a
short, flat, graceless shove more than a blow. But it landed, and Nerine grunted and jerked her arm back, and that
stopped the chopping.

Her stomach was the worst now, worse than her chest. It wasn't a bruise any more; it was a hole of pain that she
lay curled round.

She tried to get up anyway. Of course she did.

She grabbed the mossy trunk with both paws and hauled. Her left knee gave, and she dropped back onto her upper back
and hissed. She hauled again and got further. The knee gave again, and she dropped again, the jolt going straight
through her back. On the third try she got her right leg under her, hooked an elbow over the trunk and dragged
herself up the side of it. She stood there swaying, weight off the knee, one paw still pressed to her chest.

Nerine watched the sway: she swayed a little, caught it, and swayed again. Her whole body rose and fell with each
breath, the sides heaving.

Nerine had dried off at some point. The soak was gone from her fur, and the bind was gone from her leg. She shook
the leg out. *Whatever that was, she's done it for the last time.*

## Beat 17 — Crunch on the arm

Nerine's forearm was the thing pinning Ripples to the log, so Ripples bit it.

She meant to get the fin. Nerine twisted, and the jaws closed on the upper arm instead, which was worse for Nerine.
It was a crushing bite with all of Ripples' stubbornness behind it. The teeth sank in with a crunching grind, fur
bunched and skin stretched tight round each one. The shock ran down into Nerine's paw and up into her shoulder.

For the first time in the whole fight, Nerine squealed. *Eek—!* It was cut off short, but Ripples heard it. The arm
jerked back and Nerine wrenched against the teeth. Her body jerked with them as she pulled free and squared up at
once, feet still planted.

The ache stayed behind in the shape of Ripples' teeth.

Nerine took stock of her arm in one glance. She curled the paw, and pain went up to her shoulder. She uncurled it.
*That's going to slow the chops down. Good thing she's nearly done.*

Ripples watched her hold the arm in close and keep it out of use, a hitch at the top of each breath that she hid
quickly, and a wince that crossed her face whenever she shifted her weight. *She can be hurt. I just did it.*

Her own chest and stomach hadn't let her be since the trunk. She stood bent round them, one paw rubbing at her ribs
without her quite meaning it to.

## Beat 18 — Aqua Tail across the back

Nerine spun.

Both tails came round wrapped in water, hissing, whipped in a flat arc across Ripples' upper back. Ripples had her
arms curled over her chest and her back turned half away. She was guarding the wrong side, and it was the only side
she had left to guard.

The water itself did nothing to her. It was Water against Water: it soaked her and slid off her. The tails behind it
did plenty. They struck like a plank, and the muscles along her back seized round the blow. A squeal came through
her teeth and her spine flinched straight. The ache in her shoulder blades went up to something that would follow her
around.

She went down. The grass came up to meet her upper back, her left shoulder and her left hip, and she landed on her
back, face up. Her back arched away from the ground as soon as it touched, and she let out a short cry that cracked
in the middle.

She lay there soaked and dripping, her stomach still a hot hole under her ribs.

*Up,* she told herself. *In a minute. In a minute.*

## Beat 19 — Dragged by the tails

Nerine didn't give her the minute.

She caught both of Ripples' tails in her good paw and dragged.

Ripples went across the clearing on her back, her paws scrabbling for the grass and finding nothing. Then the grass
gave way to sand: warm on top, then cold and wet at the waterline. The edge of the shallows washed under her and
soaked her again. The wet sand scraped her upper back as she went, and her back, already hurting, went sharp and
high. She yelped, *Yip!*, and then again, sharper, as the small of her back dragged over a ridge. She arched away
from it and couldn't stop arching.

Then the boulder at the edge of the beach was there, and her head and left shoulder knocked into it as the drag
ended. It was a glancing knock, not much against the rest, and her face screwed up and she ducked from it.

She lay at the foot of the rock on her back, sand in her fur, lake water running out of her ears, staring up past
Nerine at the sky.

Her stomach was the loudest thing in her. She pressed both paws to it and it didn't help. *That's where she broke
me. That's where it'll be.*

Nerine stood over her and drew hard breaths. Her left arm hung a little crooked, held in close. Ripples saw it, and
saw the short, quick breathing, and the ribs working. *She's tired too. She's tired too.*

## Beat 20 — The coils

Nerine bent over her to grab her again.

Ripples' tails whipped up off the sand and round Nerine's neck.

They went round twice and cinched. One coil bit into the sides of her neck and the other lay across her throat,
squeezing it narrow until the ridge of her windpipe stood out under the cream fur. The fur at her throat was pushed
into a deep crease. It was a squeeze, not a blow; nothing broke and nothing bled. But the blood to Nerine's head was
held back, and the first pressure began pounding behind her eyes.

Nerine flexed her shoulders and shoved, testing it. *Oh, you little—*

Ripples tried to get up with the tails locked round Nerine's neck. Three times. On the first, her chest gave her no
air for it and she folded back down, gasping. On the second, her knee wouldn't take it. On the third she got as far
as sitting up, propped on one arm against the boulder. She was sitting with her back to the rock, her belly and
chest open in front of her, and her tails stretched up to Nerine's neck like a leash.

She didn't let go. She never let go.

Nerine took stock of her own arm. It was still hurting, and she couldn't use it to pry with. She looked down at
Ripples: one of Ripples' eyes half-closed on every breath out and opened again only by effort.

*She's got nothing left,* Nerine thought, *and she's choking me with it.*

## Beat 21 — The struggle builds

Nerine clawed at the tails round her throat and staggered.

The coils tightened again. Her neck was flattened under them, and every swallow showed as a jerk under the fur.
She huffed and planted her feet, then wrinkled her muzzle and pushed back, grunting. She twisted one way, then the
other, trying angle after angle. Her breath was quick and getting thinner. There was a roaring starting in her ears,
and the edges of the clearing were going grey.

Below her, Ripples got up.

It took three tries. Her left knee gave on the first and she came down on it hard. The jolt went into the joint and
up her spine, and a yowl cracked out of her, *Yaah*. The second try was worse: she was nearly all the way up when the
knee gave again. She came down on it, screamed, and shook all over, her breath coming in ragged pulls, and it was a
long moment before she could do anything at all. On the third she lurched up in one ugly heave, nearly overbalanced,
and caught herself on the boulder.

She stood there swaying on one leg with her tails still wound round Nerine's throat. Her back still hurt from the
drag, her chest wouldn't fill, and her knee wouldn't hold her.

The tails didn't loosen once.

Nerine saw the ears flick back with every move and the wince that came whenever Ripples shifted her weight. *She
can barely stand. How is she still holding on?*

## Beat 22 — Down to one knee

Nerine dropped to one knee, still clawing.

The coils tightened a third time. The pressure at her throat went up into something that made her eyes water and
her ears pin back. She jerked once against it and a short hiss escaped her, with no air to make it louder. Then she
thrashed: kicking, clawing at the gripping tails with her good paw, bucking her whole body against them. The sounds
coming out of her were rougher now, and her face was darkening under the fur. Her sight had greyed at the edges.

Her claws found the edge of one coil, dug in and pried.

It gave, a little. Not much, and not off, but a sliver of slack. Nerine dragged in one full, ragged breath, the
first in a long while. The roaring in her ears fell back a step.

Ripples felt the slack and couldn't take it back; she had no more strength to put into the squeeze. She held what
she had.

Her soaked fur had started to dry in the sun, she realised dimly. She hadn't noticed it happening.

## Beat 23 — Brick Break, and the surge

Nerine gave up on the tails and chopped at Ripples' head instead.

She was aiming for the side of the skull, but from her knee, with her neck held, she couldn't put her arms where she
wanted them. The chop came in low and hit Ripples on the jaw with a solid whump, like a sack dropped. Her head
snapped round and her eyes watered. The force went up into her skull and down into her throat in the same instant. A
high yelp came out of her, and her knees dipped under it.

The blow rocked her back, and her tails went slack.

Both coils slid off Nerine's neck at once, loose and useless. Nerine dragged in a breath that went down all the way,
then another.

Ripples should have fallen then. She was on one leg, her jaw was ringing, and her grip had been torn away. She had
nothing left.

Something else answered instead.

It came up out of her middle where the pain was worst: a hot, high, ringing feeling, like a held breath let go all
at once. The pain didn't stop, but it fell away behind something bigger. Her tails stopped trembling and began to
spin. Her black eyes went very bright. The fur all along her spine stood up.

Nerine saw it, and for the first time in the fight, she stepped back.

## Beat 24 — Crunch

Ripples went for the throat the tails had just left.

She lunged up under Nerine's chin, fast in a way she hadn't been since the oak. Nerine was still blowing hard and
still half-blind from the coils, and she jerked her head aside just in time. The teeth missed the throat and closed
on her right cheek instead.

It was a crushing bite with the surge behind it, harder than anything Ripples had thrown all day. The teeth ground
on hide and the force jarred Nerine's whole head and down into her neck. Her legs folded for an instant before she
forced them straight. A string of sharp barks broke out of her, *Ack, ack*. Her face screwed up and her eyes
squeezed shut.

Ripples wrenched her head round with the bite and Nerine's body followed. She went two, three steps toward Ripples,
dragged by the jaws, her feet scrambling under her to catch up with where her body had gone. When the teeth let go
she was out of place, off her line, her guard late.

She knew it. *Feet. Get your feet back. Now.*

Ripples saw her knocked out of her stance and followed her in.

The tightness in Nerine's throat was easing at last. Blood and breath came back into her head in a rush, and it hurt
almost as much as the choke had.

## Beat 25 — Ice Fang, sloppy

Ripples went for the arm she had already bitten.

Her teeth were white with frost, but the surge had put her a step ahead of her own body. She lunged too far, too
early, with her weight all wrong, and it went into the bite at half its proper force. She overbalanced on it and
had to catch herself.

It still landed, because Nerine was still staggering. The frost spent itself on Nerine's fur, but it didn't need to
get in: the bite itself was enough. The teeth sank into the same arm, on the same ache, and it was worse than the
first time. A yip and a hiss came out of her together, *Yipss*. Her knees dipped under it. The arm snatched itself in
tight against her body, the paw curling. The force went into her chest and down to her fingers as well. The bite
yanked her half a step off her line and let go.

Nerine got her feet back under her and planted them. She was square again, her guard back where it belonged. She
held the bitten arm close and kept it out of use, and her eyes had gone flat and cold.

*Enough.*

Ripples stood off balance, swaying, her tails still spinning. The surge was the only thing holding her up and she
could feel it draining.

## Beat 26 — Thrown into the oak again

Nerine tore her arm free and caught Ripples round the middle with the good one.

Ripples felt herself leave the ground for the second time that day. The clearing spun once, sky and pines and
glittering lake. Then the old oak came at her back-first, the same tree that had started all this.

Her upper back hit it first, the part that had already been hurting since the drag, and this time the hurt took
her over completely. A raw scream cracked in the middle. Her whole body clenched round her back, and it moved on
its own, past anything she could hold in. She jerked and curled in the air, and a paw scrabbled at the bark. The
small of her back hit next and went from hurting to much worse, and she let out a string of sharp barks with nothing
behind them.

Then she fell into the roots. Her stomach and chest hit the knotted ground, front-first, and both were already the
worst things in her. Neither blow started anything new. They poured into the screaming that was already happening:
a fresh jolt through the shaking, and the sound she was making caught and went higher, a thin, wavering wail,
*Iiiiih*.

She rolled onto her side, and it took her over.

She curled round her ribs with a paw clamped to them and her knees drawn up. Each breath was a short, hitching gasp
she had to fight for. Her other paw went behind her and pressed at her back, then at her ribs again, back and
forth, as if she could hold all of it at once. Her back arched away from the ground and then curled forward again,
because there was nowhere she could lie that didn't press on something that hurt. A sound came out of her on every
breath out, small and high.

The surge drained out of her. Every hurt came back at full size, all of them at once.

Nerine stood a few steps off, breathing hard, her bitten arm held against her side. It hadn't let her be since
Ripples' teeth had found it the second time. She rubbed it once, and winced when she made the paw curl.

*Stay down,* she thought, not unkindly. *Stay down.*

## Beat 27 — Aqua Tail

Ripples didn't stay down. She tried to get up, and that was what brought Nerine back over.

The tails came down wrapped in water, aimed at her head. Ripples got her chin down, and they caught her across the
back of the neck instead, the jolt going into her throat and her left ear. Lying on her side, she had nowhere to
roll. The blow drove her neck down into the knotted ground and every root under her pressed up into her. The water
did nothing to her; it was the tails that did it. They knocked her limbs loose and sent her neck skidding out across
the ground. A yowl cracked in her throat, and she coughed and swallowed hard, and swallowed again.

She was soaked once more, dripping.

She tried to rise anyway, three times. On the first, her paws skidded in the wet roots and she went down again. On
the second, she got as far as one elbow before her knee gave. The third was barely a try at all: a push that
didn't lift anything.

She lay there in the roots. Her back, her chest and her stomach all hurt at once, and she couldn't tell where one
ended and the next began. Her paw went from one to the other and couldn't settle.

Nerine's arm throbbed with every beat of her heart. She nursed it against her side and looked down. *Why won't you
stay down?*

## Beat 28 — Brick Break, and she stands

Nerine chopped at her head.

It came down on the side of Ripples' skull while she lay on her side, and it drove her head into the roots. There
was nowhere for it to go. The crack of it carried across the water, and birds went up out of the oak. It was more
shock than pain at first: her muzzle wrinkled hard and her teeth showed, and then the pain arrived and kept arriving.
It jarred her cheek and the back of her neck. A sharp, startled yelp came out of her. Her whole body shuddered along
the ground and came to rest a little further on.

Then she got up.

She shouldn't have. There was nothing left in her to do it with, and she did it anyway. On the first try her knee
and her right arm gave and she dropped. On the second she got almost all the way up, then had no air, and folded
back down, gasping. On the third she heaved herself up in one ugly lurch that nearly sent her over, and stood.

She was swaying. One paw was pressed to her chest and her weight was off the knee. Her head hung, and came up late.
Her black eyes found Nerine and stayed on her.

Nerine stared at her.

*Oh, you stubborn—* She couldn't decide how the thought ended. Something like respect had got into it.

## Beat 29 — The last bite

Nerine was so tired she nearly fell into the bite herself.

She went for the back of the neck. Her lunge was sloppy, badly timed, and too far. She overbalanced on it, and her
teeth found Ripples' right shoulder instead of her neck. It wasn't much of a bite: a wet clack of teeth closing.
Ripples arched away from it with a startled little *Pip*.

The force of it jarred her chest, her ruined chest. That was what did it.

A cry came out of her that jumped up in pitch halfway, *Aaah-eeh*. All of her clenched round the hurt. Her legs
buckled, and she caught them under her somehow.

Then her knees folded. She tried once more to stay up. It was the same stubborn push she had made all afternoon:
her paws reached for the ground, her chin came up, and her tails twitched.

And then everything let go together. She sagged down onto her side in the grass and didn't move again. Her eyes were
closed and her chest was rising and falling, shallow and slow. She was out cold.

The clearing went quiet.

The birds that had gone up out of the oak hadn't come back yet. The breeze came off the lake and turned the leaves
silver side up. Out on the water a fish rose. The small waves lapped at the sand.

Nerine stood over her with her arm held against her side, her breath sawing in and out. Her knee ached from the
bind. Her throat ached from the coils, and her cheek from the bite. She looked down at the small orange body curled
in the roots, at the black cheek marks and the cream-tipped tails lying still for the first time all day.

*Got you,* she thought.

It didn't feel the way she had expected. She sat down in the grass beside Ripples, carefully, favouring the arm,
and waited for her to wake up.

---

*Winner: Nerine, by knockout.*
