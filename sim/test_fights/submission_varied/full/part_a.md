## Beat 1 — first blood to the patient one

The clearing ran down to the lake in a long slope of grass, broken here and there by pine roots that had humped up
out of the ground like the backs of sleeping things. Water lapped at the sand. Two Buizel stood a few lengths apart
on the flattest bit of it, and only one of them was smiling.

Nerine was. She bounced a little on her toes, both tails flicking, and looked Ripples up and down the way you'd look
at a log you meant to roll. Ripples didn't bounce. She stood with her weight even and her forearms loose, and she
watched Nerine's throat.

It was the throat she went for. She came in low and fast and swung her right forearm across in a flat arc, the hard
blue edge of the fin leading. Nerine saw it late and jerked her chin up and away, and the fin missed the throat by a
whisker and caught her across the left shoulder instead.

It caught her perfectly. The edge bit in at exactly the angle it was built for and raked across the round of the
shoulder with a sound like a knife through wet canvas, and the shock of it ran on into the top of her back. Lake
water flew off the fin and soaked her down one side.

Nerine twisted to her right, away from it, as the rake went across her. Then she was square again, very quickly,
and she wasn't smiling any more.

*Oh,* she thought. *So that's how it's going to be.*

---

## Beat 2 — the answer is a knee

Nerine didn't bother with the throat or the face. She stepped in close, too close for another fin, grabbed a
handful of Ripples' shoulder fur for balance and drove her knee up into the meat of Ripples' left thigh.

It was a short, ugly, practical blow, and it went in deep. The big muscle at the front of the thigh took all of
it and passed the rest on into the hip above. The leg buckled a little. Ripples felt her weight slide off it and
onto the other one, all on its own, and for a moment she thought she might go down.

She didn't. The leg held, and she shoved Nerine off and got square again. But the thigh had gone hot and thick, and
when she put weight back onto it, it told her about it.

Nerine noticed. Nerine noticed everything about legs.

---

## Beat 3 — thrown on the roots

Nerine got hold of her before the thigh had stopped complaining: a fist in the fur at the back of Ripples' neck,
the other paw at her hip, and a turn of the body that used Ripples' own weight against her. Ripples' feet left the
grass. The sky swung round.

She came down on her back across one of the roots. It caught her between the shoulder blades and along the left
shoulder, the one that had already hit the ground once that day in her life and was learning to hate it, and the
air went out of her in a sharp *huk*. Her left hip hit the grass beside the root a moment later, which was softer
and didn't feel it.

She didn't roll. She didn't tumble on. She lay where she'd landed, on her back across the root with her paws open
and the pine branches turning slowly overhead, and for a little while she simply breathed.

Nerine stood off and let her. She was in no hurry at all.

---

## Beat 4 — up, and in one go

Ripples got her feet under her and pushed, and to her own surprise she came straight up in one movement, no rolling,
no paws on the grass, as if the root had never happened. The thigh grumbled about it. The shoulder said nothing
yet. She stood, and shook herself once, and set her feet.

The lake water had dried off Nerine's fur in the warm afternoon. Neither of them had taken a proper breath since the
first exchange, and they both took one now, watching each other across the grass.

---

## Beat 5 — the root trip

Nerine had been looking at the roots too.

She came in sideways, feinting high with one paw, and while Ripples' eyes went up with it Nerine's foot hooked
round behind the hurt left leg. She didn't kick it. She pulled. The knee bent the wrong way for a moment under the
strain, and Ripples' heel came up against a hump of root behind her and caught, and she went over backwards across
it.

She landed hard. The ground came up into her upper back and her left shoulder again, and into the back of her right
thigh. But this time she went with it instead of against it: she let the fall turn into a backward roll, tucked
her chin and went right over and came up onto her feet on the far side of the root, a little crooked, a little
shaky, but up.

The knee, though. The knee had been bent a way it didn't like, and it ached deep inside now, low and sullen, the way
a tooth aches before it starts to hurt properly.

---

## Beat 6 — Brick Break on the knee

Nerine didn't give it time to start hurting properly. She did it herself.

She came in again before Ripples had both feet set, raised her right paw high and brought the edge of it straight
down on the side of the left knee, the way you'd split a branch over a stone. Brick Break. The blow landed right on
the joint, and it was the hardest thing that had hit Ripples yet.

The knee lit up. That was the only way to put it. One moment it ached and the next it was full of white light, and
the light ran down into her foot and up into her hip at the same time, so the whole leg seemed to shout at once.
She didn't make a sound. She didn't have one ready. She just stood there with her mouth open and the leg trembling
under her, and she didn't let it fold.

Nerine stepped back to look at what she'd done. She looked pleased with it.

---

## Beat 7 — Ripples hits back, not quite where she meant

Ripples hated being looked at like that. She came in limping and threw a Brick Break of her own, aimed at Nerine's
left thigh, a leg for a leg, so Nerine would know what it felt like.

Nerine turned away from it. She wasn't fast enough to get clear, only fast enough to give it her back, and the
chop came down across the small of her back instead, with a hard flat *thwack* that jarred her right hip and ran
up her spine. It pushed her forward a little and arched her back away from it, and she grunted through her teeth.

She was square again in a moment. But she rubbed at the small of her back with one paw afterwards when she thought
Ripples wasn't looking, and Ripples was.

---

## Beat 8 — the fin finds the throat

This time it found what it was looking for.

Ripples stepped in and swung the fin across in that same flat, wet arc, low and fast, and Nerine was a moment late
lifting her chin. The edge raked across the front of her throat, through the cream fur and over the soft skin under
it, and the jolt carried on into the side of her neck and the top of her right shoulder. Lake water splashed off it
and soaked her again.

Nerine twisted away from the rake as it crossed her. She coughed once, a dry, startled cough, and put a paw to her
throat and took it away and looked at it, as if she expected to see something there.

*That's two,* Ripples thought. *She does guard it. She just isn't very good at it.*

---

## Beat 9 — lifted and slammed

Nerine stopped coughing and came for her.

She didn't strike. She ducked in under Ripples' arms, wrapped both of her own round Ripples' middle and lifted her
clean off the grass, legs and all, as if she weighed nothing, and for a moment Ripples hung in the air over her with
her paws beating at Nerine's back and finding nothing to do any harm with.

Then Nerine brought her down.

She slammed her onto the grass flat on her back, upper back and lower back together, all of Ripples' weight and some
of Nerine's driving her into it. The grass was soft, but not soft enough for that. It knocked every bit of breath
out of her and left her face-up under the sky with her ears ringing, trying to remember how her lungs worked.

---

## Beat 10 — the second try

The first time she tried to get up, nothing happened. Her back simply wouldn't agree to it.

So she did it the slow way. She rolled onto her front first, got her paws flat on the grass, brought her knees in
under her, the right one first and then, carefully, the left, and then pushed herself up off them. It wasn't
graceful. Her back was stiff and hot from the slam and the left knee ground when she straightened it. But she was
standing.

Nerine had waited for her again, a few lengths away, bouncing on her toes. That was starting to get under Ripples'
skin in a way the blows hadn't.

---

## Beat 11 — the knee, missed

Nerine went for the knee again. It was obvious from the moment she came in; she wasn't even hiding it any more. Her
right paw came up and chopped down at the joint, Brick Break, the same as before.

But Ripples had seen it coming, and she shifted the leg a fraction at the last moment. The edge of Nerine's paw
missed the knee and landed on her hip instead, hard, on the bone at the top of the leg, and the jolt ran down into
the thigh that was still hot from the knee earlier. It hurt. It hurt a good deal. But it wasn't the knee.

The lake water had dried off Nerine again. She shook out her paw, frowning a little, as if the hip had been
harder than she expected.

---

## Beat 12 — a blade out of the lake

Ripples backed off two steps, towards the water, and Nerine let her go, thinking she was retreating.

She wasn't. She dragged her right forearm through the shallows as she went, and drew the lake water up along the
fin with a flick of the same cold she'd have put into an Ice Fang, and the water froze where it lay. When her arm
came up out of the lake there was a blade on it: a long, thin, glassy edge of ice running the length of the fin and
a hand beyond it, beaded and smoking a little in the warm air.

Nerine's ears went flat.

Ripples went for the neck with it. Nerine ducked her head away, and the ice came down across her upper back
instead, between the shoulder blades, and sliced through the fur there with a bright cold sting she felt all the
way down into her neck. It didn't chill her. It didn't need to. She'd felt how sharp it was.

---

## Beat 13 — the bite

Nerine got inside the blade the only way she could: by going so close there was no room to swing it.

She ducked under the ice-arm, shoved her shoulder into Ripples' chest and sank her teeth into the hurt left thigh,
right into the meat at the front of it, where her knee had already been. She bit hard and held it and shook her
head once, like a dog with a rope, and the pain went up into Ripples' hip and round into the small of her back.

Ripples yelped. It came out high and short and furious, and she clubbed at the back of Nerine's head with her free
paw until the jaws came loose.

Nerine let go and stepped back, licking her teeth. There was a ragged ring of tooth marks in Ripples' thigh now,
and the whole leg was beginning to feel like it belonged to someone else.

---

## Beat 14 — the water comes back

Ripples didn't want her that close again. She dropped back a step, drew a breath and spat a hard jet of water
straight at Nerine's face and throat, a Water Gun, enough to blind her and choke her and keep her off.

Nerine met it head-on. She opened her mouth and let go a burst of Swift, a spray of bright little stars that hit the
jet in the air between them and smashed into it. The stars won. They broke the jet apart and threw it straight
back the way it had come, half the force of it and all the cold, and it hit Ripples full in the face. Water went
into both her ears, the right worse than the left, and slapped across her chest, and for a moment she couldn't hear
anything but a roaring hiss.

She shook her head hard to clear it. When she could hear again, Nerine was laughing.

---

## Beat 15 — the slam, and the blade gone

Nerine stopped laughing and lifted her again, the same way as before: in under the ice arm, both arms round the
middle, up off the grass.

Ripples knew what was coming this time, and it didn't help. The slam drove her flat on her back into the grass,
upper back and lower back together, harder than the last one if anything, and her arm flew out to the side as she
hit and the ice blade snapped off the fin with a sound like a stick breaking. It skidded away across the grass in
two glittering pieces and lay there melting in the sun.

She lay where she'd landed, face-up, winded, watching the pine branches turn. Nerine stood over her and looked
down the length of her, at the hurt left leg in particular, and Ripples saw her decide something.
