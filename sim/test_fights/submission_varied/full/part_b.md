## Beat 21 — the tail misses

Ripples stood on one leg and a half and watched Nerine circle her.

The knee was the problem now, and they both knew it. Every time Ripples put weight on it the new pain from the drop,
the sharp white one, came back. So she kept her weight on the right, and turned with Nerine, and waited.

Nerine spun and lashed both tails at the hurt shin, a crack like a whip coming low across the grass. Ripples saw the
hips turn and twisted out of the line of it, more by instinct than anything, and the tails went whistling past her
ankles and hit nothing but air.

It was the first thing all fight that had simply missed. Ripples didn't quite believe it. Nerine didn't seem to
either.

---

## Beat 22 — blocked, and a wind off the water

Ripples went back to the throat. It was the only thing she'd found that worked.

She swung the fin across, bare now without the ice, and Nerine had learned. She threw her left arm up across her
neck and took the rake on the upper arm instead, and the fin glanced off it with a dry scrape and did hardly any
harm at all. The arm was bony and hard and Nerine had braced it, and it was over before it had started.

Then a gust came up off the lake, sudden and cold, flattening the long grass in a wave and throwing a spray of
water off the surface into Nerine's back. It didn't hurt anyone. It only made Nerine flinch and glance over her
shoulder at the water, and when she looked back Ripples was still there, panting, with nothing to show for it.

---

## Beat 23 — Quick Attack into the thigh

Nerine came across the gap so fast there was no gap. Quick Attack: one moment she was three lengths away and the
next her shoulder was buried in Ripples' left thigh, right in the bitten place, with all her speed behind it.

It went into the thigh like a fist into dough, and the thigh, which had been hot and bruised and tooth-marked,
gave up being merely sore. The force ran up into the hip and down into the knee, and the knee shrieked at it.

Ripples went staggering two, three steps to her right, her hurt leg dragging and her arms out for balance. She didn't
fall. She was nearly too tired to fall properly. Her breath had gone ragged at some point without her noticing,
and the next breath was harder to find than the last one had been.

---

## Beat 24 — the third slam

Nerine caught her at the end of the stagger before she'd got her balance back, and lifted her again.

There was nothing new about it except how little Ripples could do. Her paws scrabbled at Nerine's back and slid off.
The sky swung over. The grass came up and hit her flat in the back, upper and lower, and drove what was left of her
breath out of her in a long, thin wheeze.

She lay face-up. She could feel the grass under her shoulders, and the sun, and the throbbing of the leg, and she
did not try to get up. Not yet. She was gathering herself for it, she told herself. That was all.

---

## Beat 25 — the stamp

Nerine didn't let her gather anything.

She stepped round to Ripples' left side, lifted her foot and stamped down on the hurt knee with her whole weight
behind it. Then again. The first one did it. The second one only found what the first had left.

There was a sound. Ripples heard it from very far away, inside her own leg, a wet, grinding give, and then she
couldn't hear anything because she was screaming. The knee was driven down into the grass under Nerine's heel, and
it stayed there, flat in the dent it had made, and it did not feel like a knee any more. The foot below it had gone
hot and strange and wrong.

She tried to get up. She didn't decide to; her body tried for her, three times, and each time she got about as far
as lifting her shoulders before everything below the waist refused. She couldn't even sit up. She lay on her back
in the grass with her paws clawing at the turf on either side of her, and the pain went on and on.

Nerine stood over her and looked at the knee. She looked at it for a long time.
