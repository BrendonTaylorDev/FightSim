# Ripples vs Nerine: a submission match, seed 21 (build 226)

Played on the real engine through `sim/test_fights/submission_varied/drive.py` (scene 15, the lakeside forest,
`finish.submission.enabled true`, the beat card on). These are the beats where the holds and the refusals build,
written by hand from the program's notes for each beat. The picks the program made for each refusal (the
`variety.tap_*` sets) are listed under each beat so you can see what the narrator was given.

---

## Beat 16 — the heel hook goes on

*Program: Nerine locks a heel hook: her arm round Ripples' left heel, the twist carried up into the knee, her legs
tangled round the thigh. Ripples is down, face-up.*

Ripples had been trying to get her legs under her without being noticed, a little at a time, and Nerine had seen
every bit of it. She didn't wait for the third try. She dropped onto her hip beside the hurt leg, threw both of her
own legs across Ripples' thigh and hooked the left heel into the crook of her arm, the way she might have pinned a
fish against a rock.

Then she turned it.

The twist didn't stay in the foot. It went up through Ripples' ankle as if the bones were one long rod and arrived in
her knee, which was the part Nerine had been hitting all fight. Ripples' back came off the grass in a single
hard buck and fell again. Her breath went out of her in a short, flat *hah*.

They lay at right angles to each other now, Ripples on her back with her face to the sky, Nerine on her side across
the trapped leg with her shoulder in the grass, both of Nerine's tails stretched out behind her for balance. Ripples'
free foot dug at the turf, found nothing to push against, and stopped.

---

## Beat 17 — the first refusal

*Program: the hook cranks harder; the knee goes from hurting to VERY PAINFUL. Ripples points her foot and wins a
little slack. Nerine bites her hip without letting go. Near her limit for the first time. Sign: she bites her lip to
keep the word in. Reason: the hope the grip will slip if she lasts one more breath.*

Nerine leaned back into it, and the knee answered before Ripples did: a deep, wrong pressure on the inside of the
joint, as if something in there was being asked to bend a way it had never been built for.

Ripples pointed her foot. She stiffened it from the hip down into one hard line and pulled, hoping the heel would
slide out of the crook of Nerine's arm like a wet stone. It moved. Not far, the width of a claw perhaps, but the
grip had to shift to follow it and for one breath the twist eased off.

Nerine felt it go and didn't like it. She ducked her head and bit at the nearest thing, the round of Ripples' left
hip, short and shallow because she couldn't reach it properly from where she lay. It stung more than it hurt. The
heel hook came back on a moment later, tighter than before.

The word was right there. Ripples could feel the shape of it behind her teeth, short and easy, and she set her teeth
into her own bottom lip and held them there until she tasted copper.

*It slipped once. It'll slip again.*

---

## Beat 18 — the second refusal

*Program: held 3 beats. Ripples spins, trying to roll with the twist; it gets her nowhere. Her struggle is now
DESPERATE. Sign: for a moment she stops fighting the hold at all, and everything goes into not tapping. Reason: that
it is HER. Nerine: something like respect.*

She tried rolling with it. If she could get onto her side, the way the twist ran, the knee would have somewhere to go
other than further. She threw her shoulders over and got halfway before Nerine's legs, still locked round her thigh,
dragged her flat again, and the knee took the whole of the spin.

The sound she made wasn't one she'd heard herself make before. It started low and climbed and broke apart at the
top.

After that she lay still. It wasn't surrender. Every muscle in her had simply been called off the hold and given a
different job, and the job was her paws. She kept them flat on her own chest, one over the other, and pressed down
as if they might get away from her.

*Not to her. Anyone else. Not to her.*

Through her own arm, Nerine felt the struggle stop and waited for the tap that ought to come next. It didn't. She
looked down the length of the trapped leg at Ripples' face, the eyes screwed shut, the jaw working, and something
in her own chest went quiet for a moment.

"You've done enough," she said, low. It came out sounding more like a fact than a taunt.

Ripples didn't answer.

---

## Beat 19 — the third refusal

*Program: held 4 beats. Ripples kicks with the trapped leg itself. Sign: tears running and she doesn't notice.
Reason: nothing left but a wordless plea for it to stop on its own. Nerine: coldly; it only means the hold needs
more.*

The kick came out of nowhere, even to Ripples: the trapped leg jerked inside the hook, short and stupid, the only
thing it could still do. It bought her nothing. The heel stayed exactly where it was, and the knee paid for the
kick.

Somewhere along the way her eyes had started to stream. She didn't know when. The wet ran down both sides of her
face into the fur under her ears and she didn't wipe it, didn't blink it away, didn't seem to know it was there at
all. Her mouth was open. Her breath came in short pulls that caught at the top.

There was no reason left in it now. She wasn't thinking about the slack from before, or about Nerine, or about
anything except the one thing her body was asking, which was *please*, and not to Nerine either. To the knee,
maybe. To the whole fight. *Just stop. Just let it stop by itself.*

Nerine watched the tears and adjusted her grip by a finger's width, so the twist would run straighter into the
joint. If she was still refusing, then the hold wasn't finished. That was all it meant.

---

## Beat 20 — out of it, barely

*Program: Nerine brings one paw off for a Brick Break to the head; the hook stays. Held 5 beats. Ripples curls her
toes and locks her ankle, and at the end of the beat she wrenches free. Then three tries to get up: two fail, and on
the first drop the knee becomes EXCRUCIATING.*

Nerine took one paw off the heel, only one, and brought the edge of it down across the top of Ripples' head. The
blow drove Ripples' skull into the grass and the grass gave nothing back. Her left ear rang with it.

The twist was still there. Ripples curled her toes and locked her ankle as hard as she could, making the foot one
solid knot with nothing for Nerine's arm to turn, and when Nerine reached back for a better grip, the knot came out
of the crook of her elbow like a cork.

Ripples rolled away from her. She didn't think about it; her body did it before she could.

She got her paws under her and pushed. Halfway up, the left knee folded and dropped her. She landed square on it and
everything went white for a moment. It was a new kind of pain, sharper and higher than the long grinding ache of the
hold, and a short yelp got out of her before she could stop it. She shut her mouth on the rest.

The second try got her almost upright before the leg gave again.

On the third she rolled onto her front first and walked her paws back toward her knees, and came up slowly with all
her weight on the right leg. She stood. Her left foot barely touched the grass. Her sides were heaving.

---

## Beat 26 — the knee crank

*Program: Nerine locks a new submission: both paws twisting the left knee against the grain, her twin tails wound
round the left foot and hauling. The knee is RUINED: a scream that goes on and on.*

The knee had already been through too much. When Nerine got both paws round it and twisted against the way it
bent, there was nothing left in it to argue.

Ripples screamed. It went on longer than she would have believed she had breath for, and it cracked at the top and
kept going. Nerine's twin tails had found her left foot at the same moment and wound round it twice, and they hauled
the foot one way while the paws wrenched the knee the other, so the whole leg was being wrung like washing.

The foot went cold inside the coils, then tingling, then strangely far away. The knee didn't. The knee was the only
thing in the world.

---

## Beat 27 — the fourth refusal

*Program: held 2 beats. Ripples twists slowly, looking for the angle, and tries to spin with it; it gets her
nowhere. Sign: she is saying 'no' out loud, over and over. Reason: pride: she will not be the one who gave up.
Nerine: a worry she won't show, that something in the limb she is wrenching is about to give.*

Ripples tried to find the angle the slow way, a shoulder at a time, feeling for where the twist was running so she
could turn with it instead of against it. She found it and threw herself after it, and the tails on her foot simply
followed her round, and the knee was still caught.

"No," she was saying. She didn't remember starting. "No. No. No." She wasn't saying it to Nerine. She was saying it
to the paw that kept wanting to drift off her chest toward the grass, and to the voice in her that was so tired.

She'd given up nothing in this fight. Not one inch. She was not going to be the one who did.

Nerine heard the *no* and kept the crank on, and didn't let her face move. Under her paws, though, she could feel
the knee doing something she didn't like. There was a looseness in it that hadn't been there a beat ago, a give at
the edge of the twist, and she didn't know what it was or how much more of this it had in it.

---

## Beat 28 — the tap

*Program: Nerine brings a paw off for a tremendous Brick Break to the head, jarring the right ear and throat. The
crank goes on, held 3 beats. Ripples TAPS OUT: a paw slapping the ground again and again. She is awake. After it:
she lies curled round the hurt; Nerine stands over her.*

The chop came down across the side of Ripples' head with everything Nerine had left. It cracked against her skull
and her right ear, and the end of it caught her throat and turned her *no* into a dry, hacking cough. Her whole body
slid a little way along the grass with it. Nerine's paw went straight back to the knee.

And the knee was what did it. Not the blow. The crank came back on top of the blow, and something in Ripples simply
stopped holding.

Her paw left her chest. It hit the grass beside her, flat, and then again, and again, much harder than it needed to
be, as if she were afraid that one wouldn't count.

Nerine felt the slaps come up through the ground before she heard them. She let go at once. Both paws came off the
knee, the tails unwound from the foot, and she rolled away onto her back in the grass, panting.

Ripples didn't move away. She couldn't. She curled onto her side round the leg as far as it would let her, one paw
cupped over the knee without quite touching it, and the pain that had had nowhere to go for so long went everywhere
at once. Her breath shook coming in and shook going out. She was awake for every second of it.

After a while Nerine got up. She stood over the other Buizel, favouring nothing because nothing on her was badly
hurt, and found that she didn't feel like crowing. She'd had to take that knee apart to get the tap, and even then it
had been the knee that gave up, not Ripples.

She sat down in the grass beside her and waited for her breathing to slow.
