# Ripples vs Nerine: a submission match, all 28 beats (seed 21, build 226)

The lakeside clearing, `finish.submission.enabled true`. Every beat was played on the real engine through `drive.py`; the prose is written by hand from the engine's notes for each beat.

---

## Beat 1 — first blood to the patient one

The clearing ran down to the lake in a long slope of grass, broken here and there by pine roots that had humped up
out of the ground like the backs of sleeping things. Water lapped at the sand. Two Buizel stood a few lengths apart
on the flattest bit of it, and only one of them was smiling.

Nerine was. She bounced a little on her toes, both tails flicking, and looked Ripples up and down the way you'd look
at a log you meant to roll. Ripples didn't bounce. She stood with her weight even and her forearms loose, and she
watched Nerine's throat.

It was the throat she went for. She came in low and fast and swung her right forearm across in a flat arc, the hard
blue edge of the fin leading. Nerine saw it late and jerked her chin up and away, and the fin missed the throat by a
whisker and caught her across the left shoulder instead.

It caught her perfectly. The edge bit in at exactly the angle it was built for and raked across the round of the
shoulder with a sound like a knife through wet canvas, and the shock of it ran on into the top of her back. Lake
water flew off the fin and soaked her down one side.

Nerine twisted to her right, away from it, as the rake went across her. Then she was square again, very quickly,
and she wasn't smiling any more.

*Oh,* she thought. *So that's how it's going to be.*

---

## Beat 2 — the answer is a knee

Nerine didn't bother with the throat or the face. She stepped in close, too close for another fin, grabbed a
handful of Ripples' shoulder fur for balance and drove her knee up into the meat of Ripples' left thigh.

It was a short, ugly, practical blow, and it went in deep. The big muscle at the front of the thigh took all of
it and passed the rest on into the hip above. The leg buckled a little. Ripples felt her weight slide off it and
onto the other one, all on its own, and for a moment she thought she might go down.

She didn't. The leg held, and she shoved Nerine off and got square again. But the thigh had gone hot and thick, and
when she put weight back onto it, it told her about it.

Nerine noticed. Nerine noticed everything about legs.

---

## Beat 3 — thrown on the roots

Nerine got hold of her before the thigh had stopped complaining: a fist in the fur at the back of Ripples' neck,
the other paw at her hip, and a turn of the body that used Ripples' own weight against her. Ripples' feet left the
grass. The sky swung round.

She came down on her back across one of the roots. It caught her between the shoulder blades and along the left
shoulder, and the
air went out of her in a sharp *huk*. Her left hip hit the grass beside the root a moment later, which was softer
and didn't feel it.

She didn't roll. She didn't tumble on. She lay where she'd landed, on her back across the root with her paws open
and the pine branches turning slowly overhead, and for a little while she simply breathed.

Nerine stood off and let her. She was in no hurry at all.

---

## Beat 4 — up, and in one go

Ripples got her feet under her and pushed, and to her own surprise she came straight up in one movement, no rolling,
no paws on the grass, as if the root had never happened. The thigh grumbled about it. The shoulder said nothing
yet. She stood, and shook herself once, and set her feet.

The lake water had dried off Nerine's fur in the warm afternoon. Neither of them had taken a proper breath since the
first exchange, and they both took one now, watching each other across the grass.

---

## Beat 5 — the root trip

Nerine had been looking at the roots too.

She came in sideways, feinting high with one paw, and while Ripples' eyes went up with it Nerine's foot hooked
round behind the hurt left leg. She didn't kick it. She pulled. The knee bent the wrong way for a moment under the
strain, and Ripples' heel came up against a hump of root behind her and caught, and she went over backwards across
it.

She landed hard. The ground came up into her upper back and her left shoulder again, and into the back of her right
thigh. But this time she went with it instead of against it: she let the fall turn into a backward roll, tucked
her chin and went right over and came up onto her feet on the far side of the root, a little crooked, a little
shaky, but up.

The knee, though. The knee had been bent a way it didn't like, and it ached deep inside now, low and sullen, the way
a tooth aches before it starts to hurt properly.

---

## Beat 6 — Brick Break on the knee

Nerine didn't give it time to start hurting properly. She did it herself.

She came in again before Ripples had both feet set, raised her right paw high and brought the edge of it straight
down on the side of the left knee, the way you'd split a branch over a stone. Brick Break. The blow landed right on
the joint, and it was the hardest thing that had hit Ripples yet.

The knee lit up. That was the only way to put it. One moment it ached and the next it was full of white light, and
the light ran down into her foot and up into her hip at the same time, so the whole leg seemed to shout at once.
She didn't make a sound. She didn't have one ready. She just stood there with her mouth open and the leg trembling
under her, and she didn't let it fold.

Nerine stepped back to look at what she'd done. She looked pleased with it.

---

## Beat 7 — Ripples hits back, not quite where she meant

Ripples hated being looked at like that. She came in limping and threw a Brick Break of her own, aimed at Nerine's
left thigh, a leg for a leg, so Nerine would know what it felt like.

Nerine turned away from it. She wasn't fast enough to get clear, only fast enough to give it her back, and the
chop came down across the small of her back instead, with a hard flat *thwack* that jarred her right hip and ran
up her spine. It pushed her forward a little and arched her back away from it, and she grunted through her teeth.

She was square again in a moment. But she rubbed at the small of her back with one paw afterwards when she thought
Ripples wasn't looking, and Ripples was.

---

## Beat 8 — the fin finds the throat

This time it found what it was looking for.

Ripples stepped in and swung the fin across in that same flat, wet arc, low and fast, and Nerine was a moment late
lifting her chin. The edge raked across the front of her throat, through the cream fur and over the soft skin under
it, and the jolt carried on into the side of her neck and the top of her right shoulder. Lake water splashed off it
and soaked her again.

Nerine twisted away from the rake as it crossed her. She coughed once, a dry, startled cough, and put a paw to her
throat and took it away and looked at it, as if she expected to see something there.

*That's two,* Ripples thought. *She does guard it. She just isn't very good at it.*

---

## Beat 9 — lifted and slammed

Nerine stopped coughing and came for her.

She didn't strike. She ducked in under Ripples' arms, wrapped both of her own round Ripples' middle and lifted her
clean off the grass, legs and all, as if she weighed nothing, and for a moment Ripples hung in the air over her with
her paws beating at Nerine's back and finding nothing to do any harm with.

Then Nerine brought her down.

She slammed her onto the grass flat on her back, upper back and lower back together, all of Ripples' weight and some
of Nerine's driving her into it. The grass was soft, but not soft enough for that. It knocked every bit of breath
out of her and left her face-up under the sky with her ears ringing, trying to remember how her lungs worked.

---

## Beat 10 — the second try

The first time she tried to get up, nothing happened. Her back simply wouldn't agree to it.

So she did it the slow way. She rolled onto her front first, got her paws flat on the grass, brought her knees in
under her, the right one first and then, carefully, the left, and then pushed herself up off them. It wasn't
graceful. Her back was stiff and hot from the slam and the left knee ground when she straightened it. But she was
standing.

Nerine had waited for her again, a few lengths away, bouncing on her toes. That was starting to get under Ripples'
skin in a way the blows hadn't.

---

## Beat 11 — the knee, missed

Nerine went for the knee again. It was obvious from the moment she came in; she wasn't even hiding it any more. Her
right paw came up and chopped down at the joint, Brick Break, the same as before.

But Ripples had seen it coming, and she shifted the leg a fraction at the last moment. The edge of Nerine's paw
missed the knee and landed on her hip instead, hard, on the bone at the top of the leg, and the jolt ran down into
the thigh that was still hot from the knee earlier. It hurt. It hurt a good deal. But it wasn't the knee.

The lake water had dried off Nerine again. She shook out her paw, frowning a little, as if the hip had been
harder than she expected.

---

## Beat 12 — a blade out of the lake

Ripples backed off two steps, towards the water, and Nerine let her go, thinking she was retreating.

She wasn't. She dragged her right forearm through the shallows as she went, and drew the lake water up along the
fin with a flick of the same cold she'd have put into an Ice Fang, and the water froze where it lay. When her arm
came up out of the lake there was a blade on it: a long, thin, glassy edge of ice running the length of the fin and
a hand beyond it, beaded and smoking a little in the warm air.

Nerine's ears went flat.

Ripples went for the neck with it. Nerine ducked her head away, and the ice came down across her upper back
instead, between the shoulder blades, and sliced through the fur there with a bright cold sting she felt all the
way down into her neck. It didn't chill her. It didn't need to. She'd felt how sharp it was.

---

## Beat 13 — the bite

Nerine got inside the blade the only way she could: by going so close there was no room to swing it.

She ducked under the ice-arm, shoved her shoulder into Ripples' chest and sank her teeth into the hurt left thigh,
right into the meat at the front of it, where her knee had already been. She bit hard and held it and shook her
head once, like a dog with a rope, and the pain went up into Ripples' hip and round into the small of her back.

Ripples yelped. It came out high and short and furious, and she clubbed at the back of Nerine's head with her free
paw until the jaws came loose.

Nerine let go and stepped back, licking her teeth. There was a ragged ring of tooth marks in Ripples' thigh now,
and the whole leg was beginning to feel like it belonged to someone else.

---

## Beat 14 — the water comes back

Ripples didn't want her that close again. She dropped back a step, drew a breath and spat a hard jet of water
straight at Nerine's face and throat, a Water Gun, enough to blind her and choke her and keep her off.

Nerine met it head-on. She opened her mouth and let go a burst of Swift, a spray of bright little stars that hit the
jet in the air between them and smashed into it. The stars won. They broke the jet apart and threw it straight
back the way it had come, half the force of it and all the cold, and it hit Ripples full in the face. Water went
into both her ears, the right worse than the left, and slapped across her chest, and for a moment she couldn't hear
anything but a roaring hiss.

She shook her head hard to clear it. When she could hear again, Nerine was laughing.

---

## Beat 15 — the slam, and the blade gone

Nerine stopped laughing and lifted her again, the same way as before: in under the ice arm, both arms round the
middle, up off the grass.

Ripples knew what was coming this time, and it didn't help. The slam drove her flat on her back into the grass,
upper back and lower back together, harder than the last one if anything, and her arm flew out to the side as she
hit and the ice blade snapped off the fin with a sound like a stick breaking. It skidded away across the grass in
two glittering pieces and lay there melting in the sun.

She lay where she'd landed, face-up, winded, watching the pine branches turn. Nerine stood over her and looked
down the length of her, at the hurt left leg in particular, and Ripples saw her decide something.

---

## Beat 16 — the heel hook goes on


Ripples had been trying to get her legs under her without being noticed, a little at a time, and Nerine had seen
every bit of it. She didn't wait for the third try. She dropped onto her hip beside the hurt leg, threw both of her
own legs across Ripples' thigh and hooked the left heel into the crook of her arm, the way she might have pinned a
fish against a rock.

Then she turned it.

The twist didn't stay in the foot. It went up through Ripples' ankle as if the bones were one long rod and arrived in
her knee, which was the part Nerine had been hitting all fight. Ripples' back came off the grass in a single
hard buck and fell again. Her breath went out of her in a short, flat *hah*.

They lay at right angles to each other now, Ripples on her back with her face to the sky, Nerine on her side across
the trapped leg with her shoulder in the grass, both of Nerine's tails stretched out behind her for balance. Ripples'
free foot dug at the turf, found nothing to push against, and stopped.

---

## Beat 17 — the first refusal


Nerine leaned back into it, and the knee answered before Ripples did: a deep, wrong pressure on the inside of the
joint, as if something in there was being asked to bend a way it had never been built for.

Ripples pointed her foot. She stiffened it from the hip down into one hard line and pulled, hoping the heel would
slide out of the crook of Nerine's arm like a wet stone. It moved. Not far, the width of a claw perhaps, but the
grip had to shift to follow it and for one breath the twist eased off.

Nerine felt it go and didn't like it. She ducked her head and bit at the nearest thing, the round of Ripples' left
hip, short and shallow because she couldn't reach it properly from where she lay. It stung more than it hurt. The
heel hook came back on a moment later, tighter than before.

The word was right there. Ripples could feel the shape of it behind her teeth, short and easy, and she set her teeth
into her own bottom lip and held them there until she tasted copper.

*It slipped once. It'll slip again.*

---

## Beat 18 — the second refusal


She tried rolling with it. If she could get onto her side, the way the twist ran, the knee would have somewhere to go
other than further. She threw her shoulders over and got halfway before Nerine's legs, still locked round her thigh,
dragged her flat again, and the knee took the whole of the spin.

The sound she made wasn't one she'd heard herself make before. It started low and climbed and broke apart at the
top.

After that she lay still. It wasn't surrender. Every muscle in her had simply been called off the hold and given a
different job, and the job was her paws. She kept them flat on her own chest, one over the other, and pressed down
as if they might get away from her.

*Not to her. Anyone else. Not to her.*

Through her own arm, Nerine felt the struggle stop and waited for the tap that ought to come next. It didn't. She
looked down the length of the trapped leg at Ripples' face, the eyes screwed shut, the jaw working, and something
in her own chest went quiet for a moment.

"You've done enough," she said, low. It came out sounding more like a fact than a taunt.

Ripples didn't answer.

---

## Beat 19 — the third refusal


The kick came out of nowhere, even to Ripples: the trapped leg jerked inside the hook, short and stupid, the only
thing it could still do. It bought her nothing. The heel stayed exactly where it was, and the knee paid for the
kick.

Somewhere along the way her eyes had started to stream. She didn't know when. The wet ran down both sides of her
face into the fur under her ears and she didn't wipe it, didn't blink it away, didn't seem to know it was there at
all. Her mouth was open. Her breath came in short pulls that caught at the top.

There was no reason left in it now. She wasn't thinking about the slack from before, or about Nerine, or about
anything except the one thing her body was asking, which was *please*, and not to Nerine either. To the knee,
maybe. To the whole fight. *Just stop. Just let it stop by itself.*

Nerine watched the tears and adjusted her grip by a finger's width, so the twist would run straighter into the
joint. If she was still refusing, then the hold wasn't finished. That was all it meant.

---

## Beat 20 — out of it, barely


Nerine took one paw off the heel, only one, and brought the edge of it down across the top of Ripples' head. The
blow drove Ripples' skull into the grass and the grass gave nothing back. Her left ear rang with it.

The twist was still there. Ripples curled her toes and locked her ankle as hard as she could, making the foot one
solid knot with nothing for Nerine's arm to turn, and when Nerine reached back for a better grip, the knot came out
of the crook of her elbow like a cork.

Ripples rolled away from her. She didn't think about it; her body did it before she could.

She got her paws under her and pushed. Halfway up, the left knee folded and dropped her. She landed square on it and
everything went white for a moment. It was a new kind of pain, sharper and higher than the long grinding ache of the
hold, and a short yelp got out of her before she could stop it. She shut her mouth on the rest.

The second try got her almost upright before the leg gave again.

On the third she rolled onto her front first and walked her paws back toward her knees, and came up slowly with all
her weight on the right leg. She stood. Her left foot barely touched the grass. Her sides were heaving.

---

## Beat 21 — the tail misses

Ripples stood on one leg and a half and watched Nerine circle her.

The knee was the problem now, and they both knew it. Every time Ripples put weight on it the new pain from the drop,
the sharp white one, came back. So she kept her weight on the right, and turned with Nerine, and waited.

Nerine spun and lashed both tails at the hurt shin, a crack like a whip coming low across the grass. Ripples saw the
hips turn and twisted out of the line of it, more by instinct than anything, and the tails went whistling past her
ankles and hit nothing but air.

It was the first thing all fight that had simply missed. Ripples didn't quite believe it. Nerine didn't seem to
either.

---

## Beat 22 — blocked, and a wind off the water

Ripples went back to the throat. It was the only thing she'd found that worked.

She swung the fin across, bare now without the ice, and Nerine had learned. She threw her left arm up across her
neck and took the rake on the upper arm instead, and the fin glanced off it with a dry scrape and did hardly any
harm at all. The arm was bony and hard and Nerine had braced it, and it was over before it had started.

Then a gust came up off the lake, sudden and cold, flattening the long grass in a wave and throwing a spray of
water off the surface into Nerine's back. It didn't hurt anyone. It only made Nerine flinch and glance over her
shoulder at the water, and when she looked back Ripples was still there, panting, with nothing to show for it.

---

## Beat 23 — Quick Attack into the thigh

Nerine came across the gap so fast there was no gap. Quick Attack: one moment she was three lengths away and the
next her shoulder was buried in Ripples' left thigh, right in the bitten place, with all her speed behind it.

It went into the thigh like a fist into dough, and the thigh, which had been hot and bruised and tooth-marked,
gave up being merely sore. The force ran up into the hip and down into the knee, and the knee shrieked at it.

Ripples went staggering two, three steps to her right, her hurt leg dragging and her arms out for balance. She didn't
fall. She was nearly too tired to fall properly. Her breath had gone ragged at some point without her noticing,
and the next breath was harder to find than the last one had been.

---

## Beat 24 — the third slam

Nerine caught her at the end of the stagger before she'd got her balance back, and lifted her again.

There was nothing new about it except how little Ripples could do. Her paws scrabbled at Nerine's back and slid off.
The sky swung over. The grass came up and hit her flat in the back, upper and lower, and drove what was left of her
breath out of her in a long, thin wheeze.

She lay face-up. She could feel the grass under her shoulders, and the sun, and the throbbing of the leg, and she
did not try to get up. Not yet. She was gathering herself for it, she told herself. That was all.

---

## Beat 25 — the stamp

Nerine didn't let her gather anything.

She stepped round to Ripples' left side, lifted her foot and stamped down on the hurt knee with her whole weight
behind it. Then again. The first one did it. The second one only found what the first had left.

There was a sound. Ripples heard it from very far away, inside her own leg, a wet, grinding give, and then she
couldn't hear anything because she was screaming. The knee was driven down into the grass under Nerine's heel, and
it stayed there, flat in the dent it had made, and it did not feel like a knee any more. The foot below it had gone
hot and strange and wrong.

She tried to get up. She didn't decide to; her body tried for her, three times, and each time she got about as far
as lifting her shoulders before everything below the waist refused. She couldn't even sit up. She lay on her back
in the grass with her paws clawing at the turf on either side of her, and the pain went on and on.

Nerine stood over her and looked at the knee. She looked at it for a long time.

---

## Beat 26 — the knee crank


The knee had already been through too much. When Nerine got both paws round it and twisted against the way it
bent, there was nothing left in it to argue.

Ripples screamed. It went on longer than she would have believed she had breath for, and it cracked at the top and
kept going. Nerine's twin tails had found her left foot at the same moment and wound round it twice, and they hauled
the foot one way while the paws wrenched the knee the other, so the whole leg was being wrung like washing.

The foot went cold inside the coils, then tingling, then strangely far away. The knee didn't. The knee was the only
thing in the world.

---

## Beat 27 — the fourth refusal


Ripples tried to find the angle the slow way, a shoulder at a time, feeling for where the twist was running so she
could turn with it instead of against it. She found it and threw herself after it, and the tails on her foot simply
followed her round, and the knee was still caught.

"No," she was saying. She didn't remember starting. "No. No. No." She wasn't saying it to Nerine. She was saying it
to the paw that kept wanting to drift off her chest toward the grass, and to the voice in her that was so tired.

She'd given up nothing in this fight. Not one inch. She was not going to be the one who did.

Nerine heard the *no* and kept the crank on, and didn't let her face move. Under her paws, though, she could feel
the knee doing something she didn't like. There was a looseness in it that hadn't been there a beat ago, a give at
the edge of the twist, and she didn't know what it was or how much more of this it had in it.

---

## Beat 28 — the tap


The chop came down across the side of Ripples' head with everything Nerine had left. It cracked against her skull
and her right ear, and the end of it caught her throat and turned her *no* into a dry, hacking cough. Her whole body
slid a little way along the grass with it. Nerine's paw went straight back to the knee.

And the knee was what did it. Not the blow. The crank came back on top of the blow, and something in Ripples simply
stopped holding.

Her paw left her chest. It hit the grass beside her, flat, and then again, and again, much harder than it needed to
be, as if she were afraid that one wouldn't count.

Nerine felt the slaps come up through the ground before she heard them. She let go at once. Both paws came off the
knee, the tails unwound from the foot, and she rolled away onto her back in the grass, panting.

Ripples didn't move away. She couldn't. She curled onto her side round the leg as far as it would let her, one paw
cupped over the knee without quite touching it, and the pain that had had nowhere to go for so long went everywhere
at once. Her breath shook coming in and shook going out. She was awake for every second of it.

After a while Nerine got up. She stood over the other Buizel, favouring nothing because nothing on her was badly
hurt, and found that she didn't feel like crowing. She'd had to take that knee apart to get the tap, and even then it
had been the knee that gave up, not Ripples.

She sat down in the grass beside her and waited for her breathing to slow.
