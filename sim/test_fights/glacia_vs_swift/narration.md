# Glacia vs Swift — the mountain shelf

## Beat 1 — Quick Attack

The shelf was very quiet before it started. Wind came up off the drop in long cold breaths and went over the bare
rock without finding anything to move. Glacia stood near the cliff with her wings folded. Frost had already crept a
hand's width out from her talons across the stone. She didn't look at the swallow. She looked past her, at the
cloud.

Swift couldn't keep still. She hopped sideways, then back, her forked tail flicking open and shut. Her head plumes
lifted in the gusts. She had been told the Articuno was a legend. She had decided on the way up that legends were
slow.

*Big. Pretty. Standing still. Let's see how still.*

She went without a sound. One moment she was hopping on the scree, the next she was a dark-blue streak across the
shelf, low enough that her wingtips nearly brushed the stone. She hit Glacia full in the breast with a dull, heavy
thud.

It was a solid blow, and Glacia took it the way a tree takes a gust. Her chest gave under the impact, the pale
feathers shoved flat across it. The air went out of her in one push, and her right wing jerked half open with the
jolt that ran through her. A short hiss came out through her beak, *Hss*. Then a sharp click of the beak, *Tchk*,
more irritation than pain. She drew the wing back in, one feather at a time.

Swift was already gone, flicking away across the rock, tail open like scissors.

Glacia turned her head and looked at her for the first time. Her deep red eyes were very calm.

*Fast,* she noted. *Light. She hits like a thrown stone.* The breath that went out of her came back cold, and the
frost spread a little further from her feet.

## Beat 2 — Ice Beam, and the clash

Glacia rose.

She didn't hurry. Her great translucent wings opened, glittering, and came down once, twice. Loose grit lifted off the
shelf and spun away over the edge. Then she was up, climbing in a slow spiral, her long tail streaming beneath her
like a banner. Swift stood on the rock and watched her go, head tipped back, beak a little open.

At the top of the turn Glacia looked down and opened her beak. A beam of white cold came out of her, straight as a
rod, aimed at the swallow's left wing.

Swift didn't dodge. She snapped her own wing forward and flung a blade of air up into the beam. The two met halfway
with a crack like a frozen lake splitting, and for an instant the air between them was nothing but light and flying
ice.

The beam was stronger. It tore through what was left of the air-blade and came on, thinned and weakened, and hit
her anyway.

It found her left wing first, then her wingtip and the edge of her chest, all in one crisp crackle of frost. And it
got in. The feathers over her wing stiffened and the skin beneath them pulled tight. Ice was the one thing her body
had no answer for, and she felt it go in deeper than a beam that thin should have gone. The wing jerked half open on
its own. It burned with cold, then went numb at the edges. A short hiss came out through her beak, *Hss*, and a
clipped, startled *Kek*. She flinched in on herself, every muscle round the spot going tight, and was square again
almost at once.

She shook the wing out hard. Frost flew off the blue feathers in a glittering spray.

*Cold. Okay. Okay, that's cold.* She looked up at the big bird turning lazily overhead. *Don't let her do that again.*

Glacia saw the shake and said nothing. She had seen the ice get in.

## Beat 3 — Aerial Ace

Swift went up after her.

She didn't spiral. She corkscrewed, a tight, mad twist of blue wings that took her up past the cliff's edge in two
heartbeats. Then she was above the Articuno and folding, head plumes streaming flat, coming down.

They crossed at speed. Swift aimed at the shoulder blades, but Glacia was turning, and the swallow's claws caught
her lower down, across the small of her back. There was a sound like cloth tearing. The claws caught first and the
feathers and skin dragged taut along the cut before they parted. The shock of it ran down her spine to the root of her
long tail. Then the cold air found the opened skin and made it burn. The jolt carried into her belly and her right
thigh where they hung beneath her.

Her eyes went wide, the pupils shrinking. A sharp click of the beak. She didn't make another sound, but her flight
dipped. She lost a wing's length of height before she caught it, and she favoured the spot with a slow, careful
roll that kept the hurt side away from the wind.

Then they were past each other and turning, both still up, the swallow chittering with delight.

Glacia took stock of herself in the turn. The small of her back was sore now: not deep, but there, a hot line in the
cold of her. It pulled when she beat her wings. *She aims for the high ground and takes what she can reach,* she
thought. *Then she leaves.*

## Beat 4 — Blizzard

Glacia beat her wings once, slowly. The sky between them filled with snow.

It came out of nothing: one moment open air, the next a howling white wall that swallowed the swallow whole. The
blizzard didn't strike her in one place. It was everywhere at once, and it got into all of her, every feather and
every gap between them.

Ice was what Swift's body had no answer for, and it went straight through her. It caught her across her eyes, and
she blinked and blinked and couldn't clear them. It drove into her throat so that her breath came short and
crackling. It scoured her wingtips and her forked tail and the long plumes of her head. The wind hammered her and
frost formed on her in a crisp crackling layer. Her left wingtip took the worst of it, hard enough to throw her
sideways in the white. A clipped, startled *Kek* tore out of her, and she flinched hard round the wing and
favoured it, flying lopsided.

Out of the snow she came, still up but jagged now. Her feathers were puffed out and edged with white, and she was
shaking the ice off her eyes.

*Can't see. Can't— there. There she is.*

The blizzard faded and the shelf was white with it.

Glacia hung above the swallow, quite still in the air except for the slow beat of her wings, and watched her come out
of it. She watched the feathers puffed out, the beak open, the shake.

*It reaches her,* she thought, with no satisfaction in it. *Everything I have reaches her.*

## Beat 5 — Steel Wing, dodged, and the counter

Swift came out of the white with her right wing gone hard and grey. It flashed like a blade in the thin light, and she
drove at the Articuno's left wing with it. Steel was the one thing that would bite deep into ice.

It would have, if it had landed. Glacia slipped aside. She didn't seem to move much: a tilt, a dip of one great wing.
The steel edge whistled past where her wing had been, so close the wind of it lifted her feathers.

And out of the dodge her long tail came round like a whip. It caught Swift across the belly as she went by, a
heavy, ringing blow. The swallow's body hunched round her middle. A clipped *Kek* broke out of her, and she clutched
her belly in tight to her body, shaking, flying crooked for a moment. A soreness spread through her middle and
stayed.

She was laughing when she came round, a high, breathless chirrup, but her forked tail was clamped shut.

*Missed. Ow. Okay. She's not slow. She's not slow at all.*

Her tail had begun to ache somewhere at its root, a steady pulse she hadn't noticed coming. She tested it with a
flick. It flicked, but it hurt.

Glacia watched her flying a little short and quick now, a hitch at the top of each breath that she hid at once.
*She is beginning to feel it,* Glacia thought.

## Beat 6 — Air Slash

Glacia didn't follow her. She flicked one wingtip and sent a blade of cold air after her instead.

It caught Swift across the chest and the base of her forked tail together with the shriek of sliced air. The breast
took it with a jolt that drove her breath out in a wheeze. The tail took it worse. Her eyes flew wide and a thin,
piping cry came out of her, *Kiih*. Her tail clamped down hard on its own, the two long feathers snapping shut like
closing shears.

The sting arrived a beat after the cut, sharper than the cut itself.

She flew on, but she flew differently now. She kept half-turning the hurt side away from the Articuno, as if she
could hide it in the wind. Her tail hung, the rhythm gone out of it, no longer flicking.

Swift took stock in one glance down and back. Her tail was hurting now, really hurting. It was what she steered
with, and every turn pulled at it.

*Fine,* she told herself, *fine*, and didn't believe it.

Glacia had fought this fight from a distance and meant to go on doing so. She watched the swallow turning the hurt
side away and let her.

## Beat 7 — Steel Wing on the wing, and the rake back

This time the swallow didn't miss.

She doubled back so fast she seemed to bounce off the air itself, and came in on a second pass with more speed than
the first. Her wings flashed hard and grey, and she raked the steel edge across the Articuno's left wing as she went
by.

It got in. Steel was the one thing Glacia's body had no answer for, and it went into the great translucent wing
deeper than a blow that size should have gone. Glacia's whole body went rigid for a heartbeat. Then a ragged gulp of
breath, and a harsh, cracking cry: the first sound out of her that was really a cry, *Kraa*. The wing flinched away
from the strike and her flight dipped under it. The jolt jarred into her left shoulder too. The wing went from barely
touched to very painful in one stroke, and frost sparkled off it in a loose shower.

But Glacia raked back as they crossed. Her long tail lashed out the other way as the swallow went past, in the same
instant, and caught Swift across the forked tail. It was the part that was already hurting, and it was a
tremendous blow. Swift's legs jerked up under her as though she had landed on something. A thin, piping cry
came out of her, and her tail clamped shut and stayed shut. Something in its root went from hurting to very painful.

They swung apart, both crying out, both still up.

Glacia had to beat harder now to stay where she was. Every stroke of the left wing burned. She held it in the air
anyway, flying on it the way she might have walked on a hurt leg: carefully, without complaint, losing a little height
with every beat and climbing it back.

She took stock of it calmly. *Very painful. It will not take that twice.* Across the gap the swallow was nursing her
tail, flying tight and lopsided.

## Beat 8 — Freeze-Dry

Glacia breathed out.

What came out of her wasn't a beam. It was a breath of cold so dry it smoked, and she laid it along the swallow's
right wing and across her breast like a hand stroking fur the wrong way.

Ice again, and it got in again. It bit into the right wing hard enough to make her eyes squeeze shut and her breath
come ragged. It bit harder into her chest. Her legs folded up under her for an instant before she forced them out.
Then she was gulping at the air in short, careful pulls, because her chest wouldn't open all the way. A cry was cut
off with a snap of her beak, *Kah*. Her breast, where the Quick Attack had come from at the start, was hurting now
in earnest.

Frost crackled on her feathers.

Glacia's wing still burned with every stroke. She didn't let it show in her face. It showed in the way she held the
wing, a fraction lower than the right, and in the sparkle of loose frost it shed whenever she beat it.

Swift saw it. She saw how Glacia kept half-turning the hurt side away, and how she held the left wing in close
between strokes.

*There,* Swift thought, with a bright flare of something that wasn't quite hope. *That one. I hurt that one.*

## Beat 9 — Brave Bird

Swift folded her wings and threw herself.

She came at the Articuno's breast wrapped in blue fire, and she had the speed of three passes behind her. It was
the most reckless thing she had ever done and she knew it as she did it. She hit like a meteor, with a meaty smack
that carried off across the cloud.

Glacia's chest took it. Her body jerked back in the air and her head jerked forward, and a choking cry came out of
her beak, *Kah*. The breath was knocked out of her. When it came back, it came back shallow and careful. Her
breast had gone from nothing to very painful in one hit, and the jolt had gone up into her throat and left it sore.

But the fire went back into Swift too. Brave Bird always did. The force of her own blow came straight back into
her throat with a hiss that rose into a call. Her neck went stiff and her head held very still, and a rough, raw pain
settled in her throat. Her right shoulder jarred with it.

And as she tumbled past, Glacia struck her with a short, vicious rake of talons across her right wingtip. It made her
whole body curl round the spot for a moment, and she cried out again.

Then the Articuno came down.

She had been in the air a long time on a wing that burned. She didn't fall. She settled, the hurt wing held out and
shaking, and landed on the shelf in a little cloud of frost and grit. Her talons gripped the rock. She folded her
right wing; the left she couldn't quite fold, and it hung half open beside her.

She stood very still with her chest rising and falling in short careful breaths.

*She is willing to break herself to break me,* Glacia thought. *That is worth knowing.*

## Beat 10 — Hurricane, turned back

Glacia gathered herself and turned the wind itself on the swallow.

She had too little breath for it. Her chest wouldn't fill, and the Hurricane came out of her badly timed, half the
storm it should have been. She overbalanced on it and had to catch herself with a stamp of her talons, swaying.

Swift saw it coming and did the bravest, stupidest thing again. She flung an air-blade straight into its heart.

The two winds met and Swift's was the stronger. The half-made Hurricane broke apart against it, and what was left of
it came back the way it had come and slapped into the Articuno's head. It caught her crest, her left eye and the top
of her skull in one gust, and her head ducked a moment too late. Her face screwed tight against the wind. A
sneeze-like snort, a toss of the head, a clipped, startled *Kek*. Nothing more than that, but she was off balance,
caught leaning the wrong way, and she knew it.

Swift came down out of the gust onto the scree. She had been flying for a long time, and her tail and her chest both
hurt. She landed hard and stood there panting, beak open, head plumes plastered flat with frost.

*Turned it back. Turned it right back on her.* She laughed, and it came out as a cough.

Glacia's chest hadn't let her be since the Brave Bird. She stood with it held carefully, like a thing she might spill.

## Beat 11 — Aerial Ace from above, and the strafing pass

Swift was off the scree in a flash.

Her wings snapped open and beat hard, loose grit kicked up behind her, and she climbed. The Articuno was still on the
shelf below, still off balance from the turned-back wind. She was still finding her feet when the swallow came
down out of the sky at her.

Swift was aiming at the head, but Glacia twisted her face away at the last instant. The claws took her across the
beak instead, with a sound like cloth tearing. Her eyes squeezed shut for a heartbeat and her breath went ragged.
The jolt jarred up into her crest, which was still sore from the wind. A sharp breath, *Hk*. She had been leaning
the wrong way to cover anything, and the blow went in harder for it.

Then Swift beat her wings and climbed straight back up, and her own speed carried her round. She swept back across
the Articuno for one quicker, lighter strike, a glancing rake of claws across her right wingtip. A strangled sound
came out of Glacia, and her body twisted away from it, and the swallow was gone again.

Glacia got her feet back under her, and her guard came back up with them. Her beak stung, her crest throbbed and her
wingtip smarted. Her chest still caught at the top of every breath.

She looked up at the swallow circling and thought, quite calmly: *She has found the rhythm of it. Down, and up, and
round.*

## Beat 12 — Ice Shard, cancelled

Glacia didn't move her feet. She flicked a single dart of ice up at the circling swallow's wing, fast as a thought.

Swift met it with another blade of air. They met in the sky with a sharp crack and both simply stopped. The shard
burst into glittering powder, the air-blade frayed into nothing, and nothing came through to either of them.

Snow-dust drifted down over the shelf like flour.

The two of them looked at each other through it. One was on the ground and one in the air, and both were breathing
hard. For a moment neither did anything at all.

## Beat 13 — Steel Wing on the ruined wing

Swift dropped on her from above.

She was tired now, and she knew it. Her wings were heavy and her chest was full of cold, and she came down too fast
and too early. The timing was wrong and the weight was all on the front of her. It should have been a sloppy blow.

But she brought a steel-hard wing down across the Articuno's left wing, the wing she had already hurt twice, with
the speed of two passes behind her. Sloppy didn't matter. Steel was the one thing the ice-bird's body couldn't
take, and it went into the hurt wing with a solid, sickening whump, like a sack dropped.

Glacia had known it would come there, and it still undid her for a moment.

The wing jerked away from the blow in a hard flinch she couldn't stop. A thin, piping cry came out of her before she
could hold it, *Kiih*, and was bitten off as soon as it was out. The pain went past anything she had felt in that wing.
It went past anything she had felt anywhere, and for an instant there was nothing in her mind at all but the wing.

She made herself go on. It cost her, and it showed.

Her weight went with the blow before she could stop it, several steps across the rock to her right, close to going
down. She caught herself with her good wing flung out against the stone, talons scraping. She stayed up. Only just.

Swift overbalanced on the strike, pitched forward, caught herself in the air and climbed away, wobbling.

Glacia stood with her ruined wing hanging half open at her side. Frost poured off it in a slow, steady sparkle, like
snow off a roof in the sun. Her eyes were still calm. Her breathing wasn't.

*That wing will not carry me again today,* she thought. *Then I will not need it.*

## Beat 14 — A breath

Glacia stood on the shelf with her hurt wing held half open and breathed the cold in slowly.

She didn't try to fly. She didn't attack. She watched the swallow circle above her, around and around, and let the
stagger go out of her legs. Her feet steadied on the rock, square and planted. The thin air filled her a little at a
time. Every breath caught at the top on the bruise in her chest, and she let it.

Above her, Swift got her balance back. The overreach was gone out of her flight. She circled tight and quick, her
tail held shut, her head turned always toward the white shape below.

*She's waiting for me,* Swift thought. *Fine. I'll come to her.*

A gust swept over the shelf and the stunted pines in the cracks bent and straightened.

## Beat 15 — The peck, and the catch

Swift came down at her head, beak first.

She had the speed of two passes behind her and the dive was faster than any before it. Her beak struck the
Articuno on the crown with the crack of a hard hit. Every muscle in Glacia seized and then let go in a long, ragged
gasp. Her face screwed up tight. A thin, piping cry broke out of her, and the jolt ran down into her beak. Her head
went from sore to hurting all at once. The force of it snapped her head back and turned her a single step backward,
half-turned to the swallow.

But Glacia had been waiting for exactly this. She had stood still long enough for the swallow to stop being careful.

As the beak struck, her talons came up and closed on Swift.

The swallow shrieked. The talons had her by the body, and Glacia simply pulled her out of the air. She brought her
down onto the bare rock beside her, hard, flat on her back, wings splayed. The breath went out of Swift in a squeak.
She lay looking up at the sky with her chest and belly and throat open to it, and the great ice-bird stood over her
with one talon still on her.

Glacia let go. She stepped back, half a step, and looked down.

*There you are,* she thought.

Swift lay where she had fallen and dragged at the air. She didn't try to get up. She couldn't yet.

## Beat 16 — Blizzard on the ground

Glacia spread her good wing and the sky over the shelf went white.

There was nowhere for the swallow to go. The blizzard came down on her where she lay and got into all of her at
once, in every feather. Ice was what her body couldn't bear. It caught her throat and closed it, so the cry that
came out of her was a thin, piping *Kiih* with no air behind it. Her body jerked and she tucked her head down to
guard her throat. The throat went from hurting to very painful. It hammered her right wing and both wingtips, and her
wings beat uselessly against the rock.

Then it reached her tail, the tail Glacia had already lashed twice, and it took her over.

She screamed, really screamed, a raw shriek that cracked in the middle. Her whole body clenched and curled round the
root of her tail, and it moved on its own now, past anything she could restrain. She rolled half onto her side and
back, wings scrabbling at the stone. She shook so hard she couldn't stop, and her eyes streamed. Her tail tried to
clamp shut against the pain and couldn't, the two long feathers twitching, and every twitch drew another sound out
of her. Each new place the snow found added a fresh jolt to the shaking, and the sound she was making went higher.

The snow passed. Swift lay curled in it, frosted white, shaking, her breath coming in sobs of air.

Then she tried to get up.

She beat her wings against the rock and pushed up onto her talons. Her right wing gave and she dropped. She
beat again and got nearly all the way, and the right wing gave again, and she came down hard on it. The jolt went
through the whole wing. The third time she got her feet under her and stood, unsteady, sides heaving, the right
wing held out and trembling.

Glacia watched her stand and didn't stop her.

*She will not stop,* Glacia thought. *Then neither will I.*

## Beat 17 — Brave Bird on the ruined wing

Swift dragged herself up into the air.

It wasn't flying the way she had flown at the start. Every wingbeat was an effort, ragged and labouring, and she
climbed slowly, slowly, out of reach. Glacia stood on the shelf below and watched her go up.

Then the swallow folded her wings for the last time and came down wrapped in blue fire, aimed at the ruined wing.

Glacia saw it coming and flinched before it landed. The wing snatched in a fraction too late, her whole body bracing
round it. The swallow saw her protect it and knew exactly what that meant.

Brave Bird struck the ruined wing with a dull, heavy thud, and the jolt ran out into the wingtip. A cry burst out of
Glacia that she couldn't keep in, and she bit it off as soon as it was out. The pain took her over completely. She
hugged the wing to her body, hissing through her beak, rocking with it. Then the strangest thing happened: the wing
went numb. All at once, the feeling just stopped, and that was almost worse.

The fire came back into Swift as it always did. Her own blow crashed back up her beak and into her throat. Her
throat was already the worst thing in her, and her body reacted on its own. She jerked and curled in the air, her
legs kicking at nothing, a shrill, broken wail coming out of her.

And as she streaked past, Glacia struck her again: one short slash of her talons across the swallow's throat as it
went by. It was the same hurt struck again before the last had faded, and the wail she was already making caught
and went higher.

Glacia staggered with the force of the dive, several steps toward her right and close to going down. She stayed up.

Above her, Swift should have fallen out of the sky. She didn't. Something came up out of the very bottom of her, a
hot, bright, ringing rush, and her wingbeats steadied. Her eyes went sharp. The pain didn't go anywhere, but
something bigger had stepped in front of it.

*Not done,* she thought. *Not done not done not done.*

## Beat 18 — Ice Beam, and out of the sky

Glacia looked up at her and opened her beak.

The beam went straight up into the swallow's breast. Swift was guarding her throat without thinking, a shoulder
hunched up over it, so her chest was the thing left open. Ice was what she couldn't bear, and the chest was already
badly hurt, and it took her over. She screamed again, raw, and curled round her chest in the air. Her wings stopped
beating to clutch it, and she couldn't get a breath she didn't have to fight for.

And she fell.

The beam had thrown her off her wingbeat and she couldn't catch it. She came down out of the sky and hit the bare
rock on her back. Her upper back, her right shoulder and her right thigh all struck the stone, and she cried out with
each one, and then she lay there.

Then the feeling came back into Glacia's wing.

The numbness drained out of it all at once and the pain flooded back in, as bad as it had ever been and worse for the
moment of its return. A cry came out of her that she couldn't keep in. The wing seized against her side, and the
shaking started again, all through her. Nothing new had touched her. It was the old wound, awake again.

For a long moment the two of them simply lay and stood there on the shelf, a few lengths apart, both of them hurting
and neither able to do anything about it.

Glacia's stagger went out of her slowly. She got her feet set.

## Beat 19 — Aerial Ace, blocked

Swift attacked from the ground.

She couldn't get up yet, so she didn't try. She lay on her back in the frost and lashed one claw up at the
Articuno's right wing as it came near. The surge was still in her, and it was harder than anything she'd had for
several beats.

Glacia saw it coming and turned her good wing into it, braced. The claws struck the wing instead of what they had
been aimed at, a ripping hiss, much lighter than it would have been. A snapped breath and a grimace. She pulled the
wing back.

Only then did Swift try to rise.

The first time, she got partway up and everything tilted round her, and she sank back down. The second time she got
nearly all the way. Then her right wing gave out and she dropped back hard onto it, and it went from hurting to very
painful, and she cried out. The third time she got as far as her knees, rested there one breath, and then pushed the
rest of the way. She stood swaying, her right wing held out from her body, her head plumes plastered flat.

Her chest hadn't let her be. She kept tucking her head down against it as if she could shield it with her beak.

Glacia's left wing hadn't let her be either. She stood with it drawn in close, the frost pouring off it.

## Beat 20 — Freeze-Dry

Glacia breathed out the smoking cold again, over the swallow's good wing and the root of her tail.

Swift was still guarding her throat, a shoulder hunched over it. That left everything else open, and the cold went
into the right wing and the forked tail together.

The right wing was already very painful. Now it was more than she could hold in. A raw shriek cracked in the middle,
and her whole body clenched round the wing. Her legs buckled and caught under her, and she shook and couldn't stop.
Her eyes streamed, and for a long moment there was nothing in her but that wing. And while that was still happening,
the cold reached her tail. The tail was already the worst thing in her, so it didn't start anything new. It poured
more into what was already happening: a fresh jolt through the shaking, and the shriek went higher, *KRRAAAH*. Her
tail whipped round as if to strike back at it.

She hopped off the hurt side, couldn't put her weight right, and tottered.

And then the surge drained out of her. It went all at once, the way a wave goes back, and every hurt she had came
back at full size.

She stood on the shelf with nothing holding her up but stubbornness.

*Still here,* she thought, and it was a whisper even in her own head. *Still here.*

## Beat 21 — Quick Attack, sloppy

She went for the head one more time.

It was barely a Quick Attack. It was a lurch, a stagger forward across the rock with her wings half out. She
overcommitted on it so badly she nearly went down. But Glacia was guarding her left wing without thinking,
drawn in close, and her right side was open. The swallow's body slammed into her head.

It landed like a sack dropped. A harsh, cracking cry burst out of Glacia, *Kraa*. Her mouth opened wide, her eyes
squeezed shut, and her legs dipped under her. The force jarred into her left eye, and it watered and blinked. It
jarred into her neck too, and the neck stiffened. Her head went from hurting to very painful.

The swallow tumbled off her and caught herself on the rock, off balance, gasping.

Swift's tail and her right wing hadn't let her be since the Freeze-Dry. She held them both close and kept her weight
off the hurt side.

Glacia shook her head once, slowly, and felt the ache settle in behind her eyes.

## Beat 22 — Ice Shard

Glacia flicked one more dart of ice at the swallow's chest.

Swift saw it coming at the place that was already ruined. She flinched before it landed, the chest snatched in a
fraction too late. She was still off balance from the lurch, and she couldn't get her guard where it needed to be.

The shard went into her breast with a crisp crackle of frost and it took her over. She curled round her side, a wing
clamped to her ribs. Each breath was a short, hitching gasp that she had to fight for, and then she couldn't get one
at all. Her chest heaved and nothing went in. Her beak opened on air that wouldn't come.

The pain crossed a line it had never crossed. The sound she made wasn't a scream. It was smaller than that, and
worse: a high, thin keening with no breath in it.

Glacia watched her gasp and knew there was nothing behind her blows now, not for a while.

The ache in Glacia's head hadn't let her be since the swallow's last streak. She held it very still.

## Beat 23 — Last stand

Swift beat her wing at the Articuno's right side with everything she had left.

It was a last stand and she knew it. She took the last of her breath, the last of her strength and whatever was
left under that, and put it all into one wingbeat. She had no air, and it should have been weak. It wasn't. It was
harder than anything she had thrown in a while.

Glacia got her good wing up, braced, and the blow jarred into it instead. It landed much lighter than it would have,
with a deep, slapping thud. She was driven back and had to plant herself hard not to go over. She didn't go over.

Swift's breath came back into her, ragged but real, and with it there was nothing. She had nothing left at all.

She stood swaying, emptied out, and stared at the ice-bird.

## Beat 24 — Nothing left

Glacia had nothing left either.

She stood on the shelf and dragged in air, her ruined wing hanging. Frost was still pouring off it, her chest caught
at the top of every breath, and her head throbbed. She didn't attack. She couldn't, quite.

The two of them stood a few lengths apart and breathed at each other.

The wind came up off the drop and went over them both, and neither of them moved.

## Beat 25 — The last peck, and the collapse

Swift went for the crown one last time.

She didn't fly; she couldn't. She hopped in close and jabbed with her beak, and Glacia's head turned. The peck landed
on the Articuno's left shoulder instead, a hollow thump that carried, and the force of it jarred into her ruined wing
and her neck.

The wing took it the way it had taken everything for a while now. It took her over. She hugged the wing to her
body, hissing through her beak, rocking with it. Her body twisted away to the right.

When she looked down again, the swallow had fallen over.

Nobody had touched her. Swift's body had simply given out. Her legs folded and she went down onto the rock
face-down, wings half spread, beak against the stone, her forked tail lying open and still for the first time all
day.

She was breathing. She didn't try to get up. She lay there.

Glacia stood over her.

*Stay down,* she thought. *Please.* It was the first time in the fight she had wanted anything.

## Beat 26 — Air Slash, and the knockout

Glacia flicked one last blade of air at her.

Swift saw it coming at her chest from where she lay, and flinched before it landed, her whole body bracing round it.
It caught her across the chest and the back of the neck in one stroke, with the shriek of sliced air.

And she went out.

It happened slowly, from inside. The sound of the wind drained away first, then the light, greying and narrowing.
Her legs didn't answer when she told them to fold under her. The rock came up against her breast and stayed
there. Then there was nothing. Every muscle let go at once and she didn't move again. She was out cold, still
breathing, her feathers stirring in the wind.

The shelf went quiet. Snow-dust from the last blizzard was still drifting down. The cloud below the edge moved slowly,
and the stunted pines creaked.

Glacia stood over the small blue body for a long time.

Her left wing burned with every beat of her heart. Her chest caught at the top of each breath, and her head ached.
She was very cold, even for her. She looked down at the swallow's red face, the beak a little open, the long head
plumes lying flat across the stone.

*You were very fast,* she thought.

Then she folded her good wing over the swallow, carefully, to keep the wind off her until she woke.

*Winner: Glacia, by knockout.*
