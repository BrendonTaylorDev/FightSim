— Nocturne —

Nocturne made herself breathe.

Through her mouth: her nose would not do it any more. Slow. In, out. Her muzzle throbbed and her eyes kept watering, and the ledge tilted a little whenever she moved her head, so she didn't move it. She stood square on all four feet with her weight off the bitten foreleg and let her body catch up with itself.

*She has hit me in the face,* she thought, slowly, as if reading it off a stone. *Nobody hits me in the face.*

Across the rock the Buizel was doing the same thing: standing, breathing, one arm across her ruined belly and the other hanging, her tails stopped. Neither of them came forward. The cave dripped. A drop landed on the Absol's back and she felt it go all the way down through the wet fur to the skin, and hated it, and did not move.

— Ripples —

Ripples stood with her bitten arm pressed against her chest and felt it beat there, a hot, swollen, separate thing with its own heart. Her belly was worse. She kept her other hand flat on it as if she could hold the hurt in, and every breath she took pushed against her palm. But she had felt the chop land. She had felt the dark muzzle give under the edge of her hand.

*I hit her in the face,* she thought, and could hardly believe it. *I hit her in her face and she let go.*

— Nocturne —

Nocturne stood and let her breath come back.

She didn't hurry it. A fighter who goes in winded gets put on her back. She breathed through her mouth, and kept her weight on the three good legs, and let the water drip out of her ruff, and waited for the pounding in her face to slow to something she could think round.

Across the ledge the Buizel was doing her own slow arithmetic. She had her weight on the right leg. Every breath she took, her free hand pressed harder into her belly. Her tails had started turning again, very slowly, as if they were thinking about it.

*She's counting her hurts,* Nocturne thought. *Let her. It's a long list.*

— Ripples —

Ripples stood and breathed and did her own counting. The belly first, always the belly: a raw, heavy heat that sat under her ribs and flared when she breathed too deep. The left thigh, swollen tight from hip to knee, so she kept her weight off it. The bitten arm. The ear and the throat from the very first stroke, which she had almost forgotten until she swallowed.

*A long list,* she thought. *But she's breathing through her mouth. She's breathing through her mouth because I broke her nose.*
