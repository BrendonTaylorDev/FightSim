— Nocturne —

She tried to slide out. It was the smallest movement in the world.

The Buizel leaned on her chest, and the ruined chest flared so hard that her whole body jerked under the knees, one last time, and a raw sound came up through her crushed throat. Even that was far away.

She could not get a breath. The arm was across her throat and the breath would not come round it anymore, not even the thin whistling trickle. She opened her mouth and nothing came. She tried again and nothing came.

The grey closed in from the edges, and there was only a small round piece of sky left in the middle of it, very blue, with the orange shape of the Buizel against it, very dark.

The sound of the lake went away.

The weight of her own paws went away.

The pain in her throat, the one thing that had been left, went away last of all, and then there was nothing.

— Ripples —

The Absol went still under her.

Not limp all at once. Slowly. The eyes clouded and closed. The body that had been jerking with every breath stopped jerking. The paw that had been resting against Ripples' arm slid off and lay on the grass. The breath, when it came, was very slow and very shallow and did not make a sound.

Ripples took her arm off the throat.

She stayed on her knees on the Absol's chest for a long moment, swaying, looking down at the white face. Then she slid off sideways onto the grass and lay there beside her, on her back, staring up at the sky.

The clouds were drifting in from the far shore again. Their shadows slid across the clearing. The lake lapped at the sand. In the oaks, one bird, then another, began to sing.

*Done. It's done. I did it.*

She lay there in the warm grass with every part of her hurting, her chest and her stomach and her nose and both arms, and breathed, and did not try to get up.
