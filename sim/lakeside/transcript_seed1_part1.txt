  [narrator is setting the scene]

— Both —

The clearing lay open to the lake like a held breath. Tall pines and broad oaks stood round it, their roots humped up through the grass at the edges, and along the treeline a fallen trunk lay furred with moss. On the lake side the grass thinned to a beach of pale, soft sand that slid into warm shallows, and a few lengths out the water went dark and deep. It was a warm afternoon. Fat white clouds drifted over, and their shadows slid across the grass and off again. A breeze came in off the water, turning the leaves silver side up, and somewhere in the oaks a bird was singing as if nothing in the world was about to happen.

— Nocturne —

Nocturne came out of the pines with the heavy, rolling pace of her kind and stopped where the shade ended. White fur, the dark blue-grey of her face, the scythe of her horn catching the sun as she turned her head. She looked at the lake for a long moment, the way she might look at something that had insulted her, and set her broad paws on the dry grass, well back from the sand.

*Dry ground. Stay on it.*

— Ripples —

Ripples came up out of the shallows without a splash, sleek and low, orange fur flattened to her body and running with water. She walked up the sand on two webbed feet, shook herself from ears to tail-tips, and stood there dripping in the sun, her bare cream throat pale, her twin tails beginning to turn lazily behind her. The blue fins on her forearms opened and closed once.

Her black eyes went over the Absol from horn to sickle tail, then to the careful paws on the grass, and the marks on her cheeks twitched.

*Doesn't like the water. Good.*
