— Ripples —

The edges of the sky went grey and soft. Her breath went small, then smaller. The pain in her stomach went from everything to something far away, and her paws, which had been pushing at the forepaw on her belly, slid off it and lay still in the grass. Her eyes closed. She was still breathing, slow and shallow, when the rest of her went quiet.

— Nocturne —

Nocturne felt her go slack under her paws. She held a moment longer, to be sure. Then she lifted her weight off, slowly, and stepped back, and stood over the still orange shape in the grass with her head low and her flanks heaving and her own legs trembling under her.

The bird in the oaks was still singing. The lake lapped at the sand.

*Done. It's done.*

She did not feel like she had won. She felt like she had survived. She turned her head away from the Buizel and looked at the water for a long time.

Nocturne held a moment longer, felt nothing push back, and eased her weight off.
