— Nocturne —

The moment the Buizel moved again, Nocturne was on her.

She caught her across the hips with her shoulder, lifted her off her one good leg, and threw her. It was a heave with her whole body behind it, and it dropped the Buizel flat on her back in the grass at the foot of the pine. Before she could do anything at all, Nocturne was over her. A forepaw ground down into the ruined stomach. A hind paw pinned the left thigh. Her own chest and the thick white ruff pressed down on the cream chest, heavy and immovable.

*Stay down. Stay down now.*

— Ripples —

The forepaw on her stomach was the worst thing that had ever happened to her.

It did not strike. It did not need to. It simply pressed, steady and heavy, down into the place where all of it had gone, and every breath she took pushed her stomach up into it. So she breathed less. And less.
