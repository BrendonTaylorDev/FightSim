# STORY PASSAGES: short, finished showpieces (two to four sentences) for the big moments of a fight: a heavy blow
# landing, a cut, a bite, a choke, a hold, a pin, a landing, a knockout, and for flyers a blow on a wing or a beak,
# a fall out of the air and a tumble down locked together.
#
# HOW THEY ARE USED (narration -> passages in rules.json). Now and then (not every beat: "chance", and never two
# beats running: "min_gap_beats") the program finds the biggest moment of a part and offers ONE passage that fits
# it exactly: the part of the body, how hard it landed, how badly it hurts afterwards, her body, the kind of move.
#   - Most of the time it is offered as INSPIRATION: the narrator reads it for its senses and rhythm and tells the
#     moment its own way. A sentence it copies from it anyway is cut.
#   - Sometimes ("place_share") it is offered to be SET IN as it stands, word for word, in place of the narrator's
#     own telling of that moment.
# A passage that has been offered is not offered again for a long time ("cooldown_beats"), and the least-offered
# come first; what has been offered is remembered between fights in passages_memory.json (delete it to reset).
#
# FORMAT: as story_blocks.txt. "== category | condition: values" starts a group; each line under it is one passage;
# a passage can add its own conditions after a " | " at its end. Slots: {part} (the body part, lower case, e.g.
# "left shoulder"), {foe} (the one doing it to her). "she" and "her" are always the one it happens to, except in
# the "pinning" and "holding" groups, where she is the one doing it.
# Conditions: size, level, zone, has, state, kind, held, fade (the same words as story_blocks.txt), limb ("wing"
# or "beak": the part struck is one), air ("gone":
# a choke held long, with narration.full_chokes on) and choke (the kind of choke, as the engine reads the grip:
# "blood" squeezes the sides of the neck, she still breathes but her head fills and greys; "air" shuts the windpipe;
# "both" for jaws or hands closed round the throat).
#
# EVERY PASSAGE MUST BE TRUE WHENEVER ITS CONDITIONS HOLD. So: no new events (she does not fall, step, stagger or
# get free unless the group is about exactly that), nothing beyond the hard rules (no gaping wounds, no breaking
# bones; a choke may cut her air or blood off completely, "air: gone", only with narration.full_chokes on, and
# only the choke_out group has her pass out), no weapon the move may not use (a cut is "it", not "claws"), and no
# body part she may not have (paws, ears, fur only under a has: condition; the program also drops any passage that
# names a part her body lacks).

The ground came up to meet her far faster than she was ready for. She hit it with a force that drove every bit of air out of her body at once, and lay there in the ringing silence after, her mouth open and her chest locked, waiting for her lungs to remember what they were for.
For a moment there was nothing under her at all, only air and the sickening sense of the world turning. Then the ground struck her, all along her side at once, a great flat blow that jarred through every bone she had, and she lay stunned with the shock still humming in her.
She landed hard and the impact went through her like a bell struck. Pain came from everywhere at once, her side, her shoulder, her hip, until she could not tell where it was worst; then her breath came back in a ragged gasp and the world swam slowly into place.
The landing knocked the sense out of her for a heartbeat. When it came back, she was on the ground, a deep, jarring ache spreading through her, and the world above her was tilting slowly back to where it should be.
She hit the ground with a heavy, graceless thud, and the jolt of it burned all along her side. For a breathless instant she could not move at all; then everything hurt at once, and she dragged in air like someone surfacing from deep water.

== knockout
The pain did not get worse; it simply got farther away. Sound went first, the world going muffled and soft, and then the light drew in from the edges like a tide going out, and the last thing she knew was the strange, heavy peace of letting go.
Something in her let go all at once. The fight, the hurt, the will that had kept her going this long, all of it slipped quietly out of her, and her body went slack and still, and her eyes slid closed.
She tried to hold on, and for a moment she did; then the darkness at the edges of her sight rose up and took her gently, and everything went quiet.
There was no last effort, no final defiance. The focus simply went out of her eyes, slowly, and her body sagged and settled as though a string that had been holding it up had quietly been cut.
