# Group C: new confirmed bugs (numbered from C7)

C7. A BLOCKED blow can still be DEVASTATING and still inflicts its move status. engine.py:1090-1096 rolls `_devastating_roll` and multiplies powers in the same branch that handles a guard block (only dodge/deflect/clash skip it); engine.py:1167 `_status_from_move` also runs on a block. Repro t26.py (block_chance 50, devastating chance 1, Dark status 1.0): screen shows "DEVASTATING x2.13 ... the perfect angle and timing" next to "Ripples blocks ... lands there instead, at x0.45" (71% damage on a block), and "Ripples is flinched" although "she caught it on her guard". Fix: `dev=None` when guard kind == "block"; skip _status_from_move (or roll at a reduced chance) when blocked.

C8. down.spring_up (rules.json:1393-1394, "a fighter knocked OR thrown down may come straight back up") never fires for a plain knockdown. director._landing (director.py:1136) passes `can_recover=thrown and not manual`, and engine.land (engine.py ~3290) only tries spring_up/roll_through when can_recover. Repro t27.py (evasion 0, 40 seeds, fresh fighter, Slash launch=...): "knocked down" -> 4/40 on her feet (these are guard blocks), "thrown"/"launched" -> 20/40. Fix: can_recover = (thrown or launch == "knocked down") and not manual (and let roll_through stay thrown-only if wanted), or correct the comment.

C9. evasion.base leg penalty counts a biped's ARMS as legs. engine.py dodge_chance (~2112): `legs = [... body_region(p.name) in ("hind_up","hind_low","fore_low","tail")]`. For Ripples (biped) fore_low = Forearm Fin / Paw, so ruined paws and fins cut her dodge exactly like ruined feet (rules comment: "hurt legs, feet, and tails"). Repro t29.py: fresh 0.18; paws+forearm fins at 400% -> 0.102; feet+knees at 400% -> 0.102. Fix: only include fore_low for quadrupeds (use self.body_plan(d)). Severity: minor/wrong story (the notes say her arms are hurt, not why she is slow).

C10. A "breather" never rests. director.resolve calls `engine.get_active(att)` for every action (director.py:682), and get_active marks her `involved` (engine.py:396). In _tick_status_energy (engine.py:5327) the rest_bonus (+10 energy, "if they sat the beat out") is only for fighters NOT involved, and the per-beat exhaustion health cost (engine.py:5253) only for involved ones. So the fighter who takes the breather gets +5 (same as one who fought) and still pays -0.5 health "exertion". Repro t31.py: energy 3 -> 8 -> 13 -> 18 over three breather beats (+5 each, expected +15), screen shows "Nocturne -0.5% exertion" each beat. Fix: for act == "breather" without a hold/pin, remove att from engine.involved (or don't add it in resolve).

C11. The damage roll (damage.roll +-17%) is applied silently and never shown: engine._apply_damage (engine.py:427-429) rescales `power` and returns the rolled value as h["power"], display.py:178-190 prints that as "power X" in every hit row, but the header "Power **70** = base 35 x 1 x 2" and the jar line "power 17.5" show the unrolled number. Repro t20.py: header Power 70 -> Chest row "power 63.1", jar "power 17.5" -> row "power 17.43"; a landing "(heavy, power 27 each)" lists rows 24.88 / 27.87 / 30.86 "= power N x ..." with no roll factor anywhere. The working on screen can't be reconciled by the reader. Fix: return the roll factor in the hit dict (e.g. h["roll"]=round(r,3)) and print "x0.90 roll" in the damage row (display.py ~180), or show rolled power in the header.

C12. A fighter knocked/thrown/launched out of the air stays AIRBORNE. engine.knock_down (engine.py ~2370) and engine.land never clear the "airborne" status (only grounded-by-wing-damage at engine.py:1203 and director "land" do). Repro t38.py / t39.py (Talon takes off, Seraphina's Hydro Pump with launch "thrown" + landing): in 30 seeds Talon is airborne afterwards every time: 19 times she is also in engine.downed (lying on the grass AND flying), 11 times the screen says "Talon lands hard ... rolls through it and comes up on her feet" while the notes' POSTURE line says "ends IN THE AIR". Consequences: she can't be pinned/grabbed/thrown ("Talon is in the air: no holds or pins", seen in fuzz3 seed 37), gets the airborne dodge bonus and close moves can't reach her, so a downed bird is untouchable on the ground. Fix: in knock_down()/land() (any fall, including roll-through) pop "airborne" (and report a "comes down" event) unless the blow was a mere stagger.

C13. engine.lying_at (the scenery a fighter crumpled at after a charge) is never cleared when she gets up or goes down somewhere else. Set at engine.py:1631, cleared only when a pin starts (engine.py:4905), read by get_up_tick (engine.py:2660). Repro t41.py: Take Down charge_into "an old oak" -> crumple; she rises; later knocked down on "the beach sand" -> screen: "Ripples gets up on the second try (using an old oak; climbs what she leans on)". The oak can even have broken or be across the arena. Fix: pop lying_at[name] in get_up_tick when she rises (and in knock_down/land when she goes down at a new place; land() could set it to the last upright surface instead).

C9 addendum: the same dodge-leg list counts an avian's Wingtips (body_region fore_low) as legs (Talon), so a wing-tip injury slows her dodge like a hurt foot, while ruined Wings (fore_up) do not.

## Health below 0%
Consistent with the design. Health has no floor (health.minimum null) and the fight ends only by pin or pass-out: _put_out/pinfall, escape_base (halves again every 5 points below zero), fade_gain below_zero_mult, hold_break_chance below_zero_mult, recover_tick all handle it, and every strength() consumer clamps (dodge, get-up, chain break, pin_chance). Nothing crashes or ends the fight at <= 0; fuzz of 700 beats found no negative-health invariant break. NOTE for the "-106%" log: health is absolute points shown with a "%" sign (display.pct docstring: "Health is itself a percentage (1800%)"). Ripples starts at 1700, so -106% is -106 points, about -6% of her real health, i.e. just under zero, not a hundred percent past it. Tiers (spent / past the limit) use the true share (_health_pct), so the stat block and the tiers disagree on what "%" means. Cosmetic.

- Chain: a dodged link ends the chain (t36), chain-break dodge works for throw, status-move and strike links (t33/t34), halved dodge on the next link, juggle (launch + follow-up) lands harder, landing credited as spiked.
- Statuses: paralyzed/stiff/burned/off_balance/dazed/fury/bound/adrenaline/soaked-electric power mults show in the Power line; flinched/asleep/frozen refuse director actions with a clear error; burn ticks and shows on screen and in notes; poison ticks.
- limb_mult: ruined tail/jaw/horn/paw/forearm/thigh each cut that move to x0.6 at 300% and are named on screen.
- Clash (through / back / cancel) resolves and reaches screen and notes; Protect/guard paths don't crash.
- Soaked + Electric x1.5; Water move on soaked.
- /scale, /resscale, /vital, /wear, /tumble, /healthloss commands change live rules and report.
- 700+ fuzzed beats (Nocturne/Ripples, Talon/Seraphina/Vesper/Blaze mixes, launches, landings, feints, sustain, charges, pins) found no exception; state invariants (facing for downed, pressed-and-down, holds by eliminated, negative energy) clean except C12.
