# GROUP D report (in progress)
## D1 (wrong story) Water, sand and roots "break" like furniture
Repro: audit_D/t3.py (forces chance) / break_chance() in natural play gives 0.15. engine.py:1477 break_chance(): hazards with no "break" key (the shallows, the deep water, the soft beach, the roots; any `cushion` hazard) fall to the rules table default 0.15. A charge/sustain/pin into "the deep water" or "the soft beach" can therefore "BREAK" ("the deep water lies broken", she goes down with it, later charges say "already lies broken"). Fix: hazards flagged cushion (or lacking "break" while having no `upright`) get break 0 in break_chance (or _charge/_sustain skip break roll). Also cushion surfaces take the full heavy slam.

## D2 (wrong story / director told a thing the engine refuses) Breaking one prop marks unrelated features "broken"
Repro: audit_D/t7.py (all scenes) and t8.py (frozen lake). engine.py is_broken() (~line 1433) compares only the LAST WORD of the asked surface with any word of any "lies broken" mark. So after "an ice pillar" breaks, "the thin ice" / "the bare ice" (the floor) also read as broken; charging into the thin ice is then refused ("already lies broken"), sustain/grind against it loses its surface. Same collisions: waterfall gorge (half-sunk rock <-> mossy rock), ruined temple (toppled pillar section -> carved pillar; altar stone <-> mossy stone), mountain shelf (boulder at the edge <-> the edge), lakeside (boulder at the edge of the beach -> the soft beach, a boulder), tidal beach (rock pool's edge -> barnacled rock), wreck (starboard/barnacled rail), caldera/desert (rim of X <-> X), crystal mine (cart rails -> ore cart), clock tower (bell frame -> great bell), frozen lake (ice cliff -> thin/bare ice). Fix: store the exact broken feature name (canonical prop/hazard name via scene_prop_for/hazard_for) and compare canonical names, not last word / any word.

## D3 (wrong story, silently ignored field) pinned_against / grind / dunk resolve a feature name to the WRONG prop, or to nothing
Repro: audit_D/t10.py, t11.py, t12.py. engine.scene_prop_for() (engine.py ~1447) matches when the LAST WORD of any prop appears anywhere in the asked text, and director._pin_against (director.py ~2063) tries it BEFORE the hazard. The scene brief tells the LLM to use the HAZARD names in pinned_against/charge_into, so:
 - tidal beach "the rock pool" -> a barnacled rock (the dunk never happens: no soaking/sputtering, pin "jammed against the rock"); the dunk pin-shape itself sets pinned_against to that pool name (director.py ~882).
 - caldera "the hot spring" -> "the rim of the hot spring" (no dunk); crystal mine "the cart rails" -> the ore cart; ruined temple "the mossy stone" -> the altar stone, "a toppled pillar section" -> carved pillar; lakeside "the soft beach" -> "a boulder at the edge" (pin against sand grinds her into a boulder).
 - lakeside "a boulder" (hazard, break 0.05) matches no prop: a strike/pummel/pin with pinned_against "a boulder" does NOTHING (no grind, no jam) - the model bug in the brief, again, for hazard-named solids (sea cave stalactites, caldera obsidian blades, frozen icicles, desert cactus stand, clock tower gears/bell/whistle/clock face, lakeside boulder: scene_prop_for returns None for all).
Fix: resolve the asked surface by hazard_for first (exact name/word match), then props; make scene_prop_for accept hazards flagged upright/with a break value; don't match on the last word alone.

## D4 (cosmetic, latent: only when rules.json moves.same_type_bonus != 1.0) Improvised moves get STAB in the power but it is not shown
Repro: audit_D/t15.py. engine.py move_math (~line 853): `effective` is computed with `stab`, and only afterwards `if move.get("improvised"): stab = 1.0`. With same_type_bonus 1.5 an improvised Water move vs Dark: effective 90 (=30x2x1.5) but reported stab 1.0 / no "(same type)" factor; the jar/spill code (lines 1058, 1067) then uses math["stab"]=1.0 so the carry-over power disagrees with the main hit. Fix: zero stab before computing effective.

## D5 (wrong story, minor) A held stream that runs out of breath after its first blast still "holds" and knocks her down the scenery
Repro: audit_D/t17.py (Nocturne energy 20, Hydro Pump sustain 4, pinned_against "an old oak"). engine._sustain (engine.py ~1674-1715): when energy is short the pulse loop breaks with zero extra pulses (held=1), but `back` is still set so `knock_down(... "was driven down against {against}")` runs, and display/notes say "Held for 1 pulses ... pressing her into an old oak" and tell the narrator "keeps pouring into Ripples for 1 pulses (several seconds) ... she slides down an old oak". Nothing pressed her (no oak damage rolled). Fix: if no extra pulse ran, return a plain-hit result (no held note, no knock_down); also pluralisation "1 pulses".

## D6 (cosmetic/silent) /hazard <event> for a "who: down" event with nobody on the ground is accepted and then does nothing
Repro: audit_D/t22.py, t23.py. play.py ~3282 prints "At the end of the next beat: a wave washes up." but engine._scene_event_tick (engine.py ~1561-1572) finds an empty `pool` for who "down", returns [] and the forced event is consumed with no message. Affects sea cave "a swell floods the ledge", storm forest "the wind carries spores across", mud swamp "a gas pocket bursts", tidal beach "the backwash drags", lakeside "a wave washes up" (all who:"down"). Fix: on a forced event with no eligible victim, fall back to anyone (the command always lands, like other commands) or tell the user it fizzled; for random rolls the "something happens" roll note is also logged though nothing happens.

## D9 (director told something the engine refuses) Big-moment hints ignore flight
Repro: audit_D/t27.py (Talon airborne vs Ripples). clinch_hint, grapple_hint (throw/slam) and charge_hint (director.py ~2400-2450, ~2410, ~2440 candidate loops) never check engine.has(x, "airborne") for attacker or target. With Talon in the air: every clinch/grapple idea ("TAKES HOLD of", "SEIZES") is impossible (engine: "Talon is in the air: no grabbing until she comes down" / "nobody can throw her"), and ~56% of charge ideas are Ripples CHARGES Talon (engine: "Talon is IN THE AIR: Aqua Jet can't reach her up there"). The LLM follows the hint ("take it unless the story clearly calls for something else") -> invalid action -> ValueError -> retry/wasted beat. Fix: filter airborne fighters out of options/foes (a ground fighter can only be offered sustain/ranged vs an airborne foe; an airborne attacker only charge/dive/ranged), and offer a "take off"/"land" idea instead.

## D11 (cosmetic / wrong story) Arena-event marks and labels
Repro: audit_D/t30.py (sea cave, shake_chance 1, charge into the cave wall).
 a) engine._scene_event_tick (engine.py ~1596-1597) builds the standing mark from the EVENT NAME: "a stalactite falls has left its mark on the cave" (likewise "the pendulum sweeps through has left its mark on the clock tower", "the roof sheds stones...", 12 events in scenes.json have solid+ severity and no "mark").  c) display.py:1126 labels a charge-shake event "(your /hazard)" because the shake sets engine._forced_event (engine.py ~1337), which the display reads as the user's command. Fix: carry a separate "shake" flag.

## D12 (wrong story, latent: only with /learn'd moves; none in the shipped movesets) Melee moves typed "spread"/"whole_body" count as ranged
Repro: audit_D/t32.py (/learn Ripples Thrash; Talon airborne). engine.is_ranged() (engine.py ~3137) treats every spread/whole_body move as a beam unless moves.json sets "ranged": false, and only Close Combat does. So Thrash, Spin Attack, Outrage, Petal Dance, Play Rough, Brutal Swing, Bulldoze, Earthquake (ground quake) reach an AIRBORNE fighter ("only beams, blasts and streams reach her"), can be CLASHED head-on with a beam, and count as "ranged" for range_hint/director.move_range. Fix: add "ranged": false to those moves (and let Ground moves like Earthquake/Bulldoze miss a flyer).
