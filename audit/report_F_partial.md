Talon takes off, set_status(Talon,"frozen",3) (a freeze from Ice Beam does the same). posture_text says "IN THE AIR (wings beating, above the ground), FROZEN in ice", she is not in downed, and Nocturne's Slash is refused ("IN THE AIR: Slash can't reach her up there") although she is helpless.
